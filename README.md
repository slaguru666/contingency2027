# Contingency 2027 — Convention Scenarios

Working repository for Tim Evans' games at **Contingency 2027**.

**Convention:** Contingency (Wyrd Sisters Games Club) · **Wed 27 – Sun 31 January 2027**
· Searles Leisure Resort, Hunstanton · five days of gaming
· Members-only event (£10/yr, £5 concession for GMs & volunteers)
· Advance game booking via **Warhorn**, with on-the-day sign-ups at the event
· Self-catering on-site accommodation, check-in from Mon 25 Jan

**GM commitment:** **twelve games** across fifteen slots (morning / afternoon /
evening, three per day). Friday morning, Saturday morning and Sunday evening are
deliberately clear.

**The shape of the slate:** **nine systems across twelve games** — three Cthulhu
by Gaslight, one 1920s Call of Cthulhu, one modern-day Call of Cthulhu, two
Blade Runner, and one each of The Walking Dead Universe, ALIEN, Vaesen, The Dee
Sanction and Outgunned. It is the broadest slate the convention has had and the
most expensive to prepare: five of the twelve are now the only game on the slate
in their system, so nothing shares a rules refresher.

The three Gaslight games are deliberately not three of the same thing: **The
London Particular** is mobile and outdoors across a whole city, and the other
two are each a single room or site.

### THE PERPETUAL ORDER
*Cthulhu by Gaslight (1895) · Wed morning · 4 players*

A banker's order has paid the rent on vault 114 at a Wapping bonded wharf every quarter since 1794. The wharf was built in 1829. Nobody has ever opened it, and between the four of you the padlock takes nine seconds. Inside are a hundred and one identical crates, and every one is the same Friday in June.

### AFTERIMAGE
*Blade Runner RPG · Wed afternoon · 4 players*

Los Angeles, 2037. A memory designer is dead and Rep-Detect has the case with a whisper attached: rogue replicant. The whisper comes from a corporation, and it is a lie of the useful kind. Nobody here gets to be right. You only get to decide who pays.

### THE SIX O'CLOCK SITTING
*Cthulhu by Gaslight (1894) · Wed evening · 4 players*

You have done eleven of these, and Mrs Veale will not be difficult. She isn't. By seven you have the cabinet, the thread and the whole shabby machine. What you do not have is how she knew the contents of a letter that was never posted.

### BLACK SUMMER: LAST FLIGHT OUT
*Walking Dead Universe · Thu morning · 5 players*

New York, six weeks after the fall. The army's last plane leaves a Brooklyn airfield at dawn and boarding needs a stamped chit with your face on it. Between the five of you, you have three. The queue at the far end is longer than anybody wants to think about.

### CONTINUOUS SERVICE
*ALIEN RPG · Thu afternoon · 5 players*

The ore hauler Keelson stopped answering eleven years ago. Your tug has the salvage contract: board her, verify the manifest, rig the tow. The manifest verifies. So does the payroll - forty-one crew, all clocked in, wages accruing since 2175, every shift paid without anyone once asking why. Somebody aboard is still filing the timesheets, and she has a reason.

### THE LONDON PARTICULAR
*Cthulhu by Gaslight (1896) · Thu evening · 5 players*

Four people have died in four weeks, each on a night of thick fog, each found in the open street with no wound on them. Nothing connects them until somebody fetches the Meteorological Office's fog register, and then it connects them perfectly: each died exactly where the fog lay thickest. Tonight's forecast is the worst of the winter, and it will not lift before dawn.

### VIVA VOCE
*Call of Cthulhu, 1920s · Fri afternoon · 5 players*

Miskatonic University, March 1927. A doctoral thesis of four hundred and six pages on the drainage of the Miskatonic valley. Three examiners have read it: one is in the Sanatorium and will not stop writing, one has gone deaf to a single specific sound, and the third is perfectly well, which is the one that should worry you. The viva is at three o'clock, and the committee must vote.

### THE CURIOUS CASE OF MR PARKER'S DIARY
*Vaesen · Fri evening · 4 players*

The Mythic North, February 1901. A sawmill parish in northern Sweden, in the fortnight of the year when the lake is thickest, and a diary whose last twenty pages are in Mr Parker's hand. He has been dead a month.

### PAPER CHILDREN
*Blade Runner RPG · Sat afternoon · 4 players*

A records clinician is dead on her own floor with fourteen years of immaculate filing around her. She sold the birth certificates of children who died in infancy to replicants who wanted to be somebody. Her killer will not be difficult. By this evening you will also have the list.

### CLOSING WEEKEND
*Call of Cthulhu, modern rules, 1986 · Sat evening · 6 players*

Camp Loon Point, Saturday 6 September 1986. The campers went home on Monday, the camp has been sold to a developer, and on Tuesday the bulldozers come for the boathouse. Eight counsellors stayed behind to close the cabins and have one last night at the lake. This is a slasher and it is played like one: the phone line turns out to be cut rather than down, the wagon will not start, and you should expect to play more than one character.

### THE FAVOUR
*The Dee Sanction · Sun morning · 5 players*

Six months ago you left a witch finder at a crossroads with a list in his hand: three villages where the evidence did not add up and the verdict came anyway. Dee took the list. Now Garratt has called in the favour, and what he wants is one name cleared - a woman he hanged in 1581 on evidence he was paid to believe.

### PROFESSIONAL COURTESY
*Outgunned · Sun afternoon · 5 players*

Atlantic City, September 1988. The Labor Day take from four casinos is in the vault at Boardwalk Fidelity until Tuesday, and you have spent eleven weeks on this: the guard rota, the alarm loop, a man inside, ninety seconds of thermal lance. What you do not have is the vault, because another crew came through the roof nine minutes ago and they look just as surprised as you do.

### Linked games

- **AFTERIMAGE** (Slot 2) and **PAPER CHILDREN** (Slot 11) share one set of four
  pregens, sheets, portraits and the chase quick-reference. They are separate
  cases in the same city and can be played in either order, or singly.
- **THE FAVOUR** (Slot 13) is a sequel to *The Princes Bride* (Continuum 2026)
  and reuses its seven agents. It stands alone for anyone who missed it.

### Parked

[`archive/`](archive/) holds ten games cut as the slate changed, plus the DEAD
AIR trilogy bible: *Another Bloody Treasure Map* (VANITY), *The Protestant Wind*
(the second Dee Sanction), *The Last Fix* and *The Sky Ride* (both Pulp), *The
New Light* (the fourth Gaslight), **all three DEAD AIR games**, and the pointer
to *CLEAN GROUND* (The Custodians / RingBRP, whose scenario lives in the RingBRP
system repo and was never copied here).

**DEAD AIR is off the slate** as of 2026-10-25 — the 1946/1962/2027 Call of
Cthulhu trilogy that held the three evening slots. All three outlines and the
trilogy bible are intact in `archive/` and could return to Slots 7, 10 and 15,
though not as three consecutive nights.

*The Curious Case of Mr Parker's Diary* came **back** from `archive/` in the
same revision and now holds Friday night.

### Per-game folder shape

```
games/slotNN-<slug>/
  README.md                 status line, file index, remaining prep
  scenarios/                the scenario document(s)
  characters/               pregens + print-ready sheets
  handouts/                 player-facing props
  reference/                GM quick-reference screens
  playtest/                 desk playtest logs and fix lists
  gm-utility/               GM console modules
  print/                    generated PDFs
```

Directories are created as their content is written; every game starts with a
`README.md` and a scenario outline.

## Status

**Framework stage.** Every game has a synopsis, design intent, plot truth, act
skeleton and open-questions list. One (AFTERIMAGE) is convention-ready and needs
only porting. **Eleven scenarios remain to be written** — and after the
2026-10-25 revision three of those eleven are brand new outlines with nothing
drafted beneath them, while the slate now spans **nine systems**, five of which
appear exactly once. See
[`docs/production-tracker.md`](docs/production-tracker.md) for the order and the
deadlines, and `docs/schedule.md` for how the load is meant to be survivable.
