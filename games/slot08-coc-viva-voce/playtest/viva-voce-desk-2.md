## Headline

**The four-player vote arithmetic is correct and the act is thirty well-spent
minutes.** Statute XI is a genuinely good obstacle, the viva-as-viva opening
works, and the vote-with-reasons is the right ending.

Two findings, and they are the same finding twice: **a pass-1 fix was added to
the arithmetic and never to the scene.**

1. **The Dean votes in an act he never appears in.** At four and five players
   he is a quarter of the committee and *the vote the table has to out-number* —
   and Act Three's text, from the opening read-aloud to the vote, **never
   mentions him once.** Pass 1's own closing note says *"it was its presentation
   that was missing, because the Dean existed only in the roster"* — and what
   was added was the sum, not the staging. He still has no line, no seat and no
   moment. *(staging — critical at four and five)*
2. **The stated price of leaving at three o'clock is no price for the two
   players most likely to leave.** *"Leaving the room costs that PC their vote"*
   — and the Keeper and the secretary **have no vote**, by design, and are the
   two players Statute XI forbids to speak. The scenario charges nothing to
   exactly the people with every reason to go. *(text hole)*

Timing is sound: measured **31 against a planned 30**, and the departure lands
naturally at the end of the viva.

## Play log — Act Three, planned 2:45 → 3:15

### Opening and the viva proper — 14 min

> *"Well. Shall we begin? Dr Keel, you're senior."*

**Running it as an actual viva for twelve minutes before anybody mentions
anything strange is the correct instruction and it is the act's whole
foundation.** Pell is excellent on hydrology; Lyle skims; nothing supernatural
is on the table. The players' own professional reflexes do the work.

⚠ **And the Dean is not in the room.** The read-aloud seats *"Pell… the
clerk… Prof. Lyle… the committee"* and gives the Dean nothing. At four players
he is one of four votes. The NPC roster has him — *"Dean Merrick Trowbridge,
61. Has not read it and will not pretend otherwise. Courteous, unhurried,
immovable"* — which is an excellent one-line essence that the act never
spends.

He needs three things the act does not give him: **a place in the opening
read-aloud**, **one line in the viva** that demonstrates he has not read the
thesis without making him a fool, and **a line at the vote** so the table hears
the weight they are out-numbering. He is the only NPC in the act with a vote
and no voice.

### Three o'clock — Crowder leaves for Thurlow

**The player who goes is the Keeper**, and that is not a coincidence: Statute XI
means he *"may not speak unless asked"*, he is the man who knows what is in the
Restricted Collection, and Agnes Thurlow has a book. A gagged player with a
useful errand will take the errand.

⚠ **And it costs him nothing the text can name.** The written price is *"leaving
the room costs that PC their vote, and at four it hands the decision to Dr
Sze."* Crowder has no vote. Neither does Veale. **The two sheets that never
drop are the two the price does not touch** — so at four players the line
*"doing the right thing costs the right thing"* is true for half the table and
empty for the other half.

**The real price is already in the act and just needs saying.** The four written
routes past Statute XI are: an examiner asks the Keeper a direct question; the
secretary writes on the minute and turns it round; someone breaks the statute
out loud; Lyle is worked beforehand. **Crowder leaving deletes route one — the
only route that carries the catalogue** — and Veale leaving deletes route two
*and* leaves the vote unminuted with Form 14 due back by five. Those are real,
procedural, period-correct costs, and they are better than a vote.

**The vote arithmetic, re-checked with him gone:** examiners present are Keel,
Ott, Sze and the Dean — the Keeper never had a vote, so **nothing changes.** The
printed sum holds: *two PC votes plus Sze beats the Dean 3–1; two alone tie it
and Lyle breaks the tie to pass.* ✔

**Had an examiner gone instead** — Keel or Ott — the arithmetic becomes 1 PC +
Sze + Dean. The Dean votes pass; if the remaining PC votes against, **Sze
decides.** *"Hands the decision to Dr Sze"* is exactly right. ✔ The printed
arithmetic survives both departures; only its stated *price* is wrong.

### Extract Four — 3 min

Triggered as written, *"only if someone asks Pell to explain chapter twelve"*.
`H3-A` handed over. **Sanity 1/1D2**, seeds `s08p2-ex4-*`, loss die
`s08p2-loss-ex4`:

| PC | Roll | Loss | SAN |
|---|---|---|---|
| Keel | `d100=79 vs 60` **FAIL** | **1** | 60 → 59 |
| Ott | `d100=42 vs 55` success | 1 | 55 → 54 |
| Veale | `d100=56 vs 65` success | 1 | 65 → 64 |

The 1D2 rolled a 1, so the failure and the successes cost the same — **which is
the correct shape for this scenario.** Extract Four is not meant to break
anybody; it is meant to be *in somebody's hand while they vote*, and
*"the vote happens anyway, in the next ten minutes, with that paragraph in
somebody's hand"* is the whole point.

**Act Three cannot cause a bout of madness** — 1/1D2 is the only check in it,
and madness needs five from one roll. Deliberate and correct: the act is a
decision, and a player in a bout of madness cannot make one.

### The vote — 6 min

Four speeches at four players — *"every **player** says which way and why"*,
including the two with no vote, which is right: Crowder is absent, so three
speeches, then Sze, then the Dean, then Lyle. **Keel and Ott voted against,
Sze with them, 3–1. Referred back.** Crowder came back through the door with
Thurlow's copy about ninety seconds after Form 14 was signed, which is the
best accident the act produced and is worth writing down as a staging note.

## Fix list

### 1. Put the Dean in the act he votes in *(critical at four and five — staging)*

**Change.** Three additions to Act Three:

- **Opening read-aloud:** *"…a window that looks down College Street to the
  bridge and the brown water going under it. **The Dean is already there, at
  the far end, with no copy of the thesis in front of him and no apparent
  intention of pretending otherwise.**"*
- **In the viva** (add to *How to run the viva*): *"**The Dean asks one
  question, early, and it is a good one** — he wants to know what the thesis is
  *for*, and he asks it courteously, and Pell answers it well. He will not
  pretend to have read it and he will not be embarrassed about that. **He is not
  a fool; he is a man protecting an institution from a document he has been
  told is dense.**"*
- **At the vote:** *"**The Dean votes to pass, and says why in one sentence** —
  a doctorate awarded, and nothing in writing that the Trustees could read. At
  four players this is the vote the table has to out-number, so the table
  should hear it in his voice rather than read it off your sheet."*

**Where.** `scenarios/viva-voce.md`, ACT THREE — Opening, *How to run the
viva*, and *The vote*.

### 2. Price the departure for the players who have no vote *(high — text hole)*

**Change.** Replace the price sentence in the *Go and take the book off Agnes
Thurlow* compromise with:

> **At five and four players, state the price before they go — and the price
> depends who goes.** An **examiner** forfeits their vote, which at four hands
> the decision to Dr Sze. The **Keeper** and the **secretary** have no vote to
> forfeit, so what they forfeit is a route past Statute XI: with Crowder out of
> the room **nobody can be asked a direct question about the Collection**, and
> with Veale out of it **there is no minute to turn round, and Form 14 is due
> back at five.** Say which route is closing as they stand up. Thurlow stays
> easy to reach; doing the right thing costs the right thing, and for these two
> the right thing is the only voice they had.

**Where.** Same file, *The vote* → compromise endings.

### 3. Keep the late return *(low — staging, and it is free)*

**Change.** Append to the same bullet: *"**Let them come back too late.** A PC
who leaves at three returns with the book about ninety seconds after Form 14 is
signed. Do not adjust the timing to be kind; the door opening on a decision
already made is the Epilogue's first line."*

### 4. State that Act Three cannot cause a bout of madness *(low)*

**Change.** Append to Extract Four: *"**1/1D2 is the only Sanity check in this
act, so no bout of madness is possible here** — deliberately. A player in a
bout of madness cannot cast a vote, and the vote is the ending."*

  cap at all.
