# VIVA VOCE — pregenerated characters

**Status: v1 — six sheets, audited.** Call of Cthulhu 7e, 1920s · Miskatonic
University, Arkham · Friday 11 March 1927 · **4–6 players** · 3h30.

Built to three goals:

1. **Rules-accurate.** Every sheet audits against the rules on disk (*Creating
   Investigators*, *Occupations*, *Skills*). Characteristics by **point buy —
   460 points across the eight characteristics, 15–90 range** (Option 4), then
   age modifiers applied and shown. Occupation points by the occupation's own
   formula, spend itemised, personal interests at INT×2 itemised. The optional
   **capped starting skills at 75%** rule is in force across the roster.
2. **A working party.** Six lanes with deliberate, stated overlap: the science
   (Keel), medicine and the Sanatorium (Ott), the argument's *structure*
   (Hallowes), the catalogue and the stacks (Crowder), the paperwork and the
   statutes (Veale), the ground and the drawings (Calloway). No essential fact
   sits on one sheet — the two **records** sheets double each other, and the two
   **examiners of the text** double each other.
3. **Wired into the vote.** Act Three is a committee vote, so every sheet has
   either a vote, or leverage over what the vote can do, or both — and a
   **Private** box phrased as a question the three o'clock sitting will force
   them to answer out loud, in a room where speaking out of turn is itself the
   cost.

> **Provenance.** First version; there are no previous sheets. Nothing here has
> been desk-playtested. Two sheet-level facts are new and are listed under
> *Hand-offs to scenario v1* — the scenario file is the authority and should
> overrule this roster wherever they disagree.

## The room at three o'clock (why each seat has standing)

This is the roster's working model of the committee. **Confirm or overrule it in
scenario v1** — it exists so that every sheet's leverage is legible, not to
pre-empt the scenario.

**Statute XI.** The Board of Examination for the degree of Doctor of Philosophy
comprises a **Chair** and **not fewer than three nor more than five Examiners**.
The Chair votes last and holds a casting vote on a tie. The **Registrar's clerk**
attends to minute and does not vote. The **Keeper of the Collection** in which
the deposit copy is lodged attends to receive the deposit and does not vote.

- **Chair:** Prof. Bertram Lyle (NPC). He intends to pass the candidate.
- **Examiners:** Dr Eleanor Sze (NPC) and the PC examiners.
- **In attendance:** Crowder (the deposit) and Veale (the minute).

**Who is permitted to speak.** The Chair opens and closes. Examiners question in
order of seniority of appointment. A junior member does not address the candidate
until the Chair invites them — and the Chair may simply not get round to it.
Persons in attendance do not speak unless asked a direct question by the Chair.
*This is era texture and it is also the Act Three obstacle: two of these six have
no voice in the room and the most dangerous information in the building.*

### Vote arithmetic at six, five and four

| Players | PC examiners | NPC examiners | PC votes of total | What it costs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **6** | Keel · Ott · Hallowes · Calloway | Sze | **4 of 5** | Nothing. The table can carry any outcome on its own; Lyle's casting vote only matters if the four split 2–2. |
| **5** | Keel · Ott · Hallowes | Sze + **the Dean** (takes the external seat) | **3 of 5** | The three PC examiners must vote together to carry anything. Lyle has two reliable votes; the table has three and no margin. |
| **4** | Keel · Ott | Sze + the Dean | **2 of 4** | **You must win Dr Sze in Act Two or you cannot carry the vote.** Two PC votes plus Sze beats the Dean 3–1; two PC votes alone tie and Lyle breaks the tie to pass. |

The two non-voting sheets never drop. They are the leverage sheets, and at four
players they are half the party.

## Build rules used (so every audit line is checkable)

- **Characteristics:** point buy, 460 points, 15–90 each (recommended minimum 40
  for INT and SIZ). Age modifiers applied after: 20s/30s one EDU improvement
  check; 40s two checks and −5 among STR/CON/DEX and APP −5; 50s three checks
  and −10 among STR/CON/DEX and APP −10; 60s four checks and −20 among
  STR/CON/DEX and APP −15. EDU checks are resolved at build time and the result
  is shown.
- **Hit Points** = (CON + SIZ) ÷ 10, rounded down.
- **Damage Bonus / Build** from STR + SIZ: 2–64 → −2/−2 · 65–84 → −1/−1 ·
  85–124 → none/0 · 125–164 → +1D4/+1 · 165–204 → +1D6/+2.
- **MOV:** 7 if both STR and DEX are less than SIZ; 8 if either is equal to or
  greater; 9 if both are greater. Then −1 (40s), −2 (50s), −3 (60s).
- **Sanity** = POW. **Magic Points** = POW ÷ 5. **Luck** = 3D6×5 (rolled at
  build). **Dodge** = half DEX. **Own Language** = EDU.
- **Skill caps:** optional capped starting skills, upper limit **75%**. The cap
  governs skills *as built from allocated points*; **Own Language is EDU by
  definition** and sits above it on two sheets (Ott 77, Calloway 79) with no
  points spent on it, which is correct and not an error.
- **No combat in this scenario.** No sheet carries a weapon and none needs one.

### What could not be verified (stated, not glossed)

- The rules on disk are **two-column OCR**, so each occupation's points/Credit
  Rating lines interleave with its neighbour's. Every occupation used here was
  attributed by matching the printed **skills list** to the occupation's own
  description: Professor (EDU×4, CR 20–70), Doctor of Medicine (EDU×4, CR 30–80),
  Librarian (EDU×4, CR 9–35), Secretary (EDU×2 + DEX or APP ×2, CR 9–30),
  Engineer (EDU×4, CR 30–60). **Mechanic/Skilled Trades, Musician and Shopkeeper
  could not be disentangled from their neighbours and were therefore not used.**
- Ch03's Luck heading reads *"Roll 2D6+6 and Multiply by 5"* while its own body
  text and the Quick Fire method both say **3D6×5**. 3D6×5 is used throughout.
- Ch05 prints Appraise at both 05% and 01%. No sheet uses Appraise.
- The **Alienist/Psychiatrist** entry exists only in the chapter index on disk,
  not as a resolvable block, which is why the physician is a Doctor of Medicine.
- Sanity-loss values, Keeper-side difficulty calls and the 1920s cost of any
  individual item are Keeper's-book material and are not part of a pregen build.

## Party seams (GM reference)

- **Keel ↔ Hark ↔ Calloway.** Three men and one absent fourth. Keel owes Hark
  his career; Calloway owes him forty years and one unanswered letter. Put them
  in the Sanatorium corridor together and let them not say it.
- **Hallowes ↔ Pell.** The referred-back candidate and the candidate about to
  be. She is the mirror. If the table is drifting toward a tidy referral, let her
  recognise the form in Veale's hand.
- **Crowder ↔ Veale.** The two who cannot speak. They will find each other
  within ten minutes, and they between them hold the catalogue, the slips, the
  register, the abstract, the statutes and the minute. **This is the pairing the
  scenario should reward.**
- **Ott ↔ Sze.** He laughed at her question about deafness. She has not
  mentioned it since. Her best scene is with him in the room.
- **Veale ↔ the thesis.** She is the only PC who has read all of it,
  attentively, start to finish — which makes her the roster's quiet horror and
  its single most dangerous Act Three statement.

## Split recommendations (nudge, don't force)

- **Act One — the department (three pairs at six players).** Keel + Calloway
  take the bound copy against the submitted abstract (binding order, the 1928
  citation). Ott + Hallowes take the Sanatorium and Hark's pages. Crowder +
  Veale take the catalogue and Pell's file. Each pair comes back with a document
  the other two pairs have not seen.
- **Act Two — library (Track A) and Sanatorium/Sze (Track B).** Crowder + Veale
  own Track A outright; that is the whole reason they exist. Keel + Ott take
  Track B. Hallowes floats — she is the one who can read Amory's notes *as a
  method* in the stacks, or stand in the corridor and hear what Sze will only say
  to a woman the university has also disappointed. Calloway goes wherever the
  drawings are.
- **Act Three — nobody splits.** One room, six chairs, four of them with votes.

## Player-count scaling

- **At five, drop sheet 6 (Calloway)** — the outline's own instruction: the
  outside vote goes to the Dean. *Cost:* the roster loses its one unpunishable
  vote and its hardest truth-teller, so the table's three remaining examiners
  must agree to carry anything. Re-home Calloway's survey material on Keel
  (Science (Geology) 75, Navigate 40) and his letter on Ott, who can be told
  about it by Sze.
- **At four, also drop sheet 3 (Hallowes)** — her lane is the recoverable one:
  the *structure* argument can be made by Keel on the hydrology and by Veale on
  the typing, and Veale already carries Psychology 45 and Listen 50 as the
  social backup. *Cost, stated plainly:* the roster loses its sharpest
  "permitted-to-speak" friction, so hand that friction wholly to Crowder and
  Veale — and **at four the table cannot carry the vote without Dr Sze**, which
  makes winning her in Act Two mandatory rather than optional. Tell the GM that
  in the scenario's pacing note.
- **Splits at four: 2+2 only, never 2+1+1.** Keel + Ott are Track B; Crowder +
  Veale are Track A. If the clock forces one pair to be alone, it is Crowder +
  Veale in the stacks, because that track has a document fallback at every step
  and Track B does not.
- **When a 2+2 has to become 2+1** (a player steps out, or one track runs long):
  split Crowder off alone in the Restricted Collection — he is the only PC whose
  lane works with nobody to talk to — and keep Veale with the examiners, where
  her minute-book gives her something to do in every scene.
- **At seven (a walk-up).** The room will not hold a seventh voice: Statute XI
  caps the Examiners at five and the roster already fills it. If a seventh player
  must be seated, move **Dr Eleanor Sze** out of the NPC column and hand her over
  as a PC. *Cost:* the scenario loses its Act Two pacing valve — Sze exists so
  Act Three is never unreachable — so the GM must re-home that job on Lyle, who
  will say the same facts for entirely self-interested reasons.

## Hand-offs to scenario v1 (new facts this roster introduces)

The scenario file is the authority. Two sheet-level facts here are not in the
outline and should be either adopted or struck:

1. **Keel saw the Dunwich Bend anomaly in 1921 and filed it as instrument
   error.** This gives the internal examiner a private stake and a confession to
   withhold. It does not change the hydrology or Pell's achievement.
2. **Crowder filled in the borrowing slip of the 6th himself, and Veale typed
   the January fair copy.** Both answer the outline's open question *"are the two
   borrowers named, or blank?"* from the PC side rather than the NPC side. If the
   scenario names a student borrower for the 6th, make Crowder's slip the **9th**
   instead and leave the 6th to the student — one word's change on his sheet.

Also assumed and in need of confirmation: **Statute XI**, the composition of the
Board, **Form 14**, the five o'clock lodging deadline, and the Dean as the
fallback external. All six sheets lean on them in Act Three.
