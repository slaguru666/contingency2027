## The agent shape, verified

Every agent, as printed: one **d8 / d6 / d4** spread across **Physicall ·
Intellectuall · Supernaturall** · **3 Hits** · **Fortune 1** · **three
Abilities** (Occupation / Association / Focus) · **one Favour** ·
**Unravelling track starting d8**. Verified against the core rulebook at desk
pass 1, along with the 1-or-2-Falters rule and the Step-Up/Step-Down ladder —
see [`../playtest/the-favour-desk-1.md`](../playtest/the-favour-desk-1.md).

> **The book calls them Abilities, not Expertise.** "Expertise" appears once in
> the rulebook, as **"Lack of Expertise"** — the GM's grounds for ruling a task
> beyond an agent who has no relevant Ability. The *Princes Bride* audit used
> the wrong word; the count of three and the Back Story pattern are right.
> **An Ability that plainly covers an ordinary, unhurried task gives the fact
> with no roll**, which in an archive scenario matters more than the dice.

**Sheet quirks kept:** the period spellings (*Intellectuall*, *Nervious*) are
charm, not errors — the book spells them that way too.
