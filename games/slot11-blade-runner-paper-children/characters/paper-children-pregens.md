# PAPER CHILDREN — pregenerated characters

**Status: v1 — port, not new writing.** The four sheets are the finished
**AFTERIMAGE** sheets, unchanged, in
[`~/Git/Continuum2026/characters/afterimage-pregens.md`](../../slot02-blade-runner-afterimage/README.md)
and its print pack. **This file is the pointer, not a second copy** — it says
what each sheet is for *in this case*, and nothing else. If a number here ever
disagrees with the AFTERIMAGE sheet, **the sheet wins.**

> **Do not re-audit the sheets against this scenario's needs and then change
> them.** They are shared with Slot 2, which runs three days earlier with the
> same players possible at both tables. Any change to a sheet goes into **both
> games or neither.**

- **System:** Blade Runner RPG — **A = d12, B = d10, C = d8, D = d6**; two Base
  Dice per roll (attribute + skill), 6+ is a success, 10+ on one die is two.
- **Table size:** 4 players. **All four sheets are in play; there is no drop
  order**, because the party is a shift and a shift of three is a different
  scenario. At three, cut **Jax** and give the freight depot to Rick — see
  *What a missing seat costs*.
- **Two humans, two replicants.** Rick and Jax are human and push once; Kael
  and Sloane are Nexus-9s, push twice, and always take stress instead of
  damage. **Say that in Slot Zero as a rules note.** It is the only mechanical
  difference between the people at the table, and this is the case about who
  counts as a person.

## The 2+2 split for Act Two

**AFTERIMAGE's own split recommendations are for AFTERIMAGE's scenes. These are
this case's.**

- **Jax + Rick → the two interviews that are conversations.** Adeyemi in her
  classroom and Oyelaran with his lawyer in the room. Jax gets them through the
  door; Rick reads what they will not say.
- **Sloane + Kael → the two that are not.** Vicario at the ambulance bay, where
  the hardest interview in the scenario is conducted on a man who is busy; and
  Lindqvist in East LA, watching a clock. **Put the two replicants on the two
  suspects who are most sympathetic.** That is the point of the pairing, and
  nobody has to say so.
- **Teale (Act Two, late) is Jax's**, via the depot, whoever else is in the car.

## Party seams in this case

- **Rick ↔ the ledger.** Forty-one names, thirty-one alive. He is the one who
  will count them out loud, and he has a daughter who does not pick up.
- **Jax ↔ Teale.** Teale is not a monster and he is cooperative and he is a
  freight dispatcher — one of Jax's own. The arrest is easy; that is the
  trouble with it.
- **Kael ↔ the Voight-Kampff.** She has sat on the other side of a machine
  like this, every month, for eleven months, and has never once deviated.
  **Give her the line about what the test is like from the chair.**
- **Sloane ↔ Grace Lindqvist's daughter.** Pilar, nine, human, conceived by a
  process on no form anywhere. Sloane's file says *donor-derived composites*.
  She will work it out and she has made a professional decision not to read
  this kind of evidence. Let her.

## What a missing seat costs

**Nothing essential lives on one sheet**, which is checked:

- The ledger is **Automatic** on reading it and needs no skill.
- Clue 6 has Sang's own cross-reference index as its fallback.
- The V-K is on **Insight**, which Rick (B/d10), Jax (C/d8) and Sloane (B/d10)
  all have — it is not Rick's alone.
- The ocular-implant route is `[BR: Medical Aid or Observation]`, which every
  sheet can attempt and two can do well.
- **At three players, cut Jax.** It costs the warrantless route to the depot,
  so Teale comes in on a request rather than a conversation, and the four
  clients take longer to reach. Give Rick the depot and move Act Two's cut list
  up one.
