# BLACK SUMMER — pregenerated characters

**Status: brief only.** No sheets written yet. Design brief below, then build.

Wire every PC into the case: each one needs a reason to be here, one thing only
they can do, and one thing they want that is not the mission. Nothing essential
may live on a single sheet — if that PC is unplayed, the game still runs.

- **System:** The Walking Dead Universe — Last Flight Out
- **Table size:** 4–6 players
- **Drop order:** <!-- which PC is cut first at the smaller table, and what
     that costs the splits -->

Five, each wired into the chit maths and to at least one other PC:

1. **The one with the child** — has a chit for the child and none for herself.
2. **The soldier** — deserted the field detachment two days ago and knows the
   layout, and one of the reasons the queue is wrong.
3. **The broker** — arrived with three chits and sold two. Knows the market.
4. **The doctor** — the only PC everyone else has a practical reason to keep alive.
5. **The liar** — has a chit that is not theirs and a face that does not match it.

Nothing essential lives only on one PC. Starting chit count: **three, whatever
the table size.** Fixed at three because the adverts say so, and because the
scene is "there are not enough" rather than a ratio. **Note the consequence
before playtesting:** three of four is a much gentler table than three of six,
so the squeeze this game is built on varies with who turns up. If that is too
loose at four, change the chits and the advert together, not just the chits.
