# MR PARKER'S DIARY — Pre-Generated Characters (6 sheets, plays at 4)

Six members of the Society, built for an archival mystery that ends on a frozen
lake at night. Three goals:

1. **Rules-accurate.** Every sheet audits clean against **Vaesen core, ch. 2
   and ch. 4** — age-banded attribute and skill budgets, the 2/4/5 attribute
   caps, the 2/3 starting skill caps, Resources started at the archetype's
   lower bound and raised only with skill points, one talent at creation. The
   budget line is printed on each sheet so a GM can re-check it in ten seconds.
   No archetype is used twice (core p. 18).
2. **A working party.** Six lanes, almost no overlap: **archive** (Etta),
   **physical evidence** (Oskar), **people and confession** (Brandt), **the ice
   and the lake** (Mats), **the diary itself** (Vivian), **the body that goes in
   after you** (Teddy). The two set-pieces are audited against the roster: the
   register hunt has three readers, the rotten-ice crossing has three bodies.
   Nobody sits skill-dead in either.
3. **Wired into the case.** Each sheet has a **Private** box. The case is a
   grievance about something owed and a parish that passed its problem to a
   stranger, so every private seam is a version of the same question —
   *what do you hand on, and to whom?* — and Act Three forces an answer.

## The headquarters layer — what a one-shot needs

Vaesen's castle and upkeep layer is a **campaign** system. The core book settles
it: *"If you are only going to play a single mystery, you can ignore the castle
completely and start on the way to the mystery"* (core p. 86). So:

- **Cut:** Development points, Upgrades, threats in Upsala, exploring the castle,
  hiring staff, the between-mysteries Insight/Defect resolution. None of it is
  referenced on any sheet.
- **Kept, because it costs nothing and buys a lot:** each PC holds one of the
  Society's six **offices** (core p. 85 — Guardian, Treasurer, Shadowmaker,
  Castle Master, Librarian, Custodian). Six offices, six sheets, no bookkeeping.
  The office is the reason this PC is the one who acts: the Librarian owns the
  register hunt, the Shadowmaker owns the telegram to Härnösand, the Treasurer
  pays for the sledges and the dean's clerk's "fee," the Guardian owns the ice,
  the Custodian owns the Fear and the Conditions, the Castle Master signed for
  the parcel.
- **Kept, 30 seconds of it:** the pre-expedition rite (core p. 85) — blessed at
  the chapel, the circle before Artemis, two fingers to the heart, a dried
  flower each for the journey. Open the Introduction with it. It is free
  atmosphere and it tells six strangers they are one body.

## Rules the table needs, printed once

- **Rolling:** attribute + skill, that many d6. **Every 6 is a success.** One
  success usually passes (core ch. 3).
- **Pushing:** after a failure you may re-roll, at the cost of one **Condition**
  (core p. 39).
- **Conditions:** physical — EXHAUSTED, BATTERED, WOUNDED (penalise Physique and
  Precision); mental — ANGRY, FRIGHTENED, HOPELESS (penalise Logic and Empathy).
  Each costs **1 die**, and they stack. You always roll at least one die. A
  **fourth** of either type = **Broken** + a critical injury (core p. 40).
- **Fear tests:** Logic *or* Empathy, your choice, **plus one die per PC present
  (max +3)**. Broken or already-Terrified PCs don't count. Successes needed =
  the Fear value: 1 scary, 2 terrible *(dead children)*, 3 unbearable
  (core p. 68).
- **Memento:** interact with it to **heal one Condition**. Explain how; the GM
  has final say (core p. 22). Six sheets = six Condition heals banked at the
  table. This is the roster's entire medical plan and it is deliberate.
- **Advantage:** one per mystery, chosen on the journey north, used once —
  **+2 dice** to one skill test, declared before the roll (core p. 24).
  Pre-chosen on every sheet; let a player swap it in Slot Zero.

> **Audit notes, v1.** Built from the rulebook, not from memory: the archetype
> table (main attribute, main skill, three talents, Resources band, equipment)
> was read off core pp. 26–35 and the talent texts off pp. 50–53. All six sheets
> were re-checked by script against the age budgets, the attribute and skill
> caps and the Resources bands — all six pass. Two things to watch at the first
> desk playtest: (a) Mats's Bloodhound is written to cover reading lake ice as
> *tracking*, which is a GM ruling, not printed text — see his sheet; (b) Vivian
> is the only sheet with a printed mechanical handle on the diary conversation,
> so the GM note under her Private box matters.

## 1. HENRIETTA "ETTA" LINDQVIST — Academic · Middle-aged (38) · **Librarian**

*She has read the last twenty pages four times, and she has not yet told anyone that she also copied them out.*

| | | | |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Physique 3** | **Precision 3** | **Logic 5** | **Empathy 3** |

*(Budget: Academic, main attribute **Logic** → cap 5; middle-aged = **14 attribute
points**, 3+3+5+3 = 14 ✓; non-main attributes ≤ 4 ✓)*

**Conditions:** Exhausted · Battered · Wounded — Angry · Frightened · Hopeless
**Resources 4 → 5** *(Academic band 4–6; one skill point spent to raise it, core p. 21)*

**Skills (above the 0 baseline):** LEARNING 3 · INVESTIGATION 2 · VIGILANCE 2 ·
OBSERVATION 2 · MANIPULATION 1 · AGILITY 1
*(Budget: middle-aged = **12 skill points**; 3+2+2+2+1+1 = 11, +1 to Resources = 12 ✓;
start cap 2, except main skill **LEARNING** at 3 ✓)*

**Dice pools:** LEARNING **8** · INVESTIGATION **7** · VIGILANCE **7** ·
OBSERVATION **5** · MANIPULATION **4** · AGILITY **4**

**Talent — BOOKWORM:** *gain +2 to LEARNING when looking for clues in books or
libraries.* In the presbytery, the mill's legal file or a dean's transcript that
is **10 dice**. She is the register.
*(Academic's other two, if a player prefers: Erudite — pass a LEARNING test to
establish a truth about a place or phenomenon, never about vaesen. Knowledge is
Reassuring — ignore Conditions on LEARNING tests.)*

**Advantage (pre-chosen):** *"I wrote to a colleague in Härnösand before we
left."* → +2 dice to one LEARNING or MANIPULATION test, once.

**Gear:** book collection, writing utensils, slide rule · travelling ink and
blotter · spectacles she cannot read without · her own fair copy of Parker's
last twenty pages, in her hand, folded into the back of her notebook.
**Memento — a map with notes in the margin,** of a lake in Jämtland she has
never been to, annotated by her late brother. *(Interact to heal one Condition.)*

**Key memory:** Six weeks of her doctoral year spent proving that a parish
register could not possibly have been destroyed deliberately, because no
registrar would. She was wrong, and it was published, and she has never been
wrong in print since because she stopped publishing.
**Key relationship: Pastor Elias Ström**, who answered her first letter about
Hällvattnet in eleven pages and her second not at all. She has the eleven pages
with her. He wrote something in them he should not have, and he knows it.

**Private (do not share):** *You copied the last twenty pages out longhand
before you showed the diary to anyone. You told yourself it was scholarship —
a document this strange should exist in two places. It took you four hours and
you did not once stop to wonder why you wanted it so badly.*

*The copy is in your notebook. You have read it more times than the original.
And somewhere in the second hour of copying you noticed that your hand had
stopped being careful and started being fluent, as though you were taking
dictation rather than transcribing, and you kept going, and you have not
written that down anywhere.*

**Playing Etta:** ask for the document before you ask the person · correct
people's facts and apologise for it immediately · when the room gets frightening
you start describing what you see in the past tense, like a footnote.

**The diary has already looked at her.** One of the two blanks on handout `H2-B`
can be filled with her.

**Playing Mats:** say the one useful sentence and stop · test everything with
your own weight before anyone else moves · you answer questions about the
wilderness immediately and questions about yourself a day late.

| Pulling someone out of the water, after | Mats, MEDICINE 7 · Brandt, MEDICINE 5 | **7 / 5** |
| Conditions, when they come | six mementos = six Condition heals | — |

Thinnest point: **Vivian, Physique 2**, who is also the sheet most likely to be
standing closest to the hole. That is on purpose. Put the line in Teddy's hands.

### Party seams (GM reference)

- **Oskar ↔ Lind, and the parents of 1897.** He wrote the report Lind wanted.
  Lind will be pleased to see him in Act One, in front of everybody.
- **Etta ↔ Ström.** He wrote something in those eleven pages he should not have.
  She has them in her bag. Let her find it, don't give it to her.
- **Brandt ↔ Ström.** Two priests who have each decided what to tell the
  bereaved. Put them alone in the presbytery for four minutes.
- **Mats ↔ Kerstin.** Shared people, shared language. He is the key to her door
  and the one person she will not perform for.
- **Vivian ↔ Parker.** The whole scenario. He is courteous, he never threatens,
  and he will ask her the thing she did not answer in October.
- **Teddy ↔ the recruitment.** He is already being shown around. He is also
  22 and sworn to someone. The ending "hand it on" has a volunteer at the table,
  and nobody knows.

### Split recommendations (nudge, don't force)

- **Act One — do not split.** One parish, one mill, one man everybody works for.
  Give Oskar the works and the outflow, Mats the lake and the sledge road,
  Brandt and Vivian the parish, Etta the presbytery, Teddy the baggage and
  every door.
- **Act Two, at six — 2+2+2.**
  - **Etta + Teddy** → the presbytery and the mill's legal file. She reads, he
    opens, lifts and carries, and Loyal keeps her rolling when it gets bad.
  - **Vivian + Brandt** → Kerstin Aho's cottage. Confessor gets her talking;
    if she will not talk, Automatic Writing asks Parker instead.
  - **Oskar + Mats** → the dammed inlet by daylight, and the 1897 file. The man
    who reads ground and the man who reads ice.
- **At five — 2+2+1.** Drop Oskar (below). The lone PC is **Mats**, at the inlet
  in daylight: it is outdoors, it is his, and it is the one Act Two scene with
  no Fear test in it.
- **At four — 2+2, and never 2+1.** **Etta + Teddy** / **Vivian + Mats**.
  The reason is mechanical: Fear tests add one die per PC present (max +3), so a
  PC alone in a Fear scene rolls a bare attribute. Two pairs, both nights.
- **If the table insists on three ways at four players,** the lone PC is Mats,
  for the reason above, and the GM should move that scene out of doors.

### Player-count scaling

**Drop order: Oskar first, then Brandt.** At four the table is
**Etta · Mats · Vivian · Teddy** — register, ice, diary, body.

- **Drop 1 — OSKAR THULIN (Private detective).** Goes first because his lane is
  the most recoverable: Etta has INVESTIGATION 7 and takes the **Shadowmaker**
  office as well as the Librarian's.
  **What it costs:** (a) the Elementary safety valve — without it, the GM must
  run the Act Two pacing note as written and let Kerstin say it straight;
  (b) the 1897 guilt seam, which is the roster's sharpest. Recover it as a
  *document*, not a confession: the "accidental" finding goes into Lind's legal
  file in Oskar's hand, signed, and one of the parents recognises the name on
  it. The parish accuses an absent man instead of a present one, which is
  weaker, and is the actual price of the smaller table;
  (c) the telegram to Härnösand. **At four players, stop making the table earn
  it** — put it on the Countdown and have the clerk's reply arrive.
- **Drop 2 — PASTOR BRANDT (Priest).** Costs the Confessor route into Kerstin,
  so her refusal must break on Vivian's MANIPULATION 7, on Mats being her kind
  of people, or on the Härnösand reply. Also costs the four-children grief its
  spokesman at the table: give those lines to **Pastor Ström**, who is already
  praying about it and only needs permission to say what about.
- **Walk-up +1 at seven?** Vaesen has no bolt-on sidekick rule and the
  no-duplicate-archetype line (core p. 18) means a seventh needs a seventh
  archetype. **Don't.** A walk-up plays **Doctor** (Logic/MEDICINE, Resources
  4–6, talent *Army Medic* — +2 Fear tests when frightened by dead or damaged
  human bodies, which is this scenario exactly), middle-aged, as the Society's
  physician who came for the drowning and not for the diary. Build it on the
  night only if you must; nothing in the roster is waiting for it.

### Open question 1 from the outline, answered

*How specific can `H2-B` — the page dated nine days ahead that describes the
players — be, when pregens are chosen in the Introduction?*

**Specific. Leave two blanks and fill them after selection.** This roster makes
that safe: three of the six sheets are already marked *the diary has looked at
you* (Etta copied it, Vivian corresponded with Parker, Teddy read it first and
alone), and all three survive the drop order to four. Print `H2-B` with two
gaps in Parker's hand and fill them in ink, at the table, in the ten-minute
break — **Vivian** first if she is in play, then **Teddy**, then **Etta**.
Nothing in the handout needs to hedge.

## Sources, and what is unverified

**Verified against the rulebook itself.** The Vaesen core book is on disk at
`Conventions/Contingency 2026/01 Wednesday/Solt 2 Afternoon Vaesen/Vaesen -
Nordic Horror Roleplaying .pdf`, and every number above was read off it rather
than recalled: the age bands and their attribute/skill budgets (core p. 18) ·
the 2/4/5 attribute caps and the 2/3 starting skill caps (pp. 20–21) · the
twelve skills and their attributes (p. 21) · Resources started at the
archetype's lower bound and raised only by skill points, never past the upper
bound (p. 21) · all ten archetypes' main attribute, main skill, three talents,
Resources band and equipment (pp. 26–35) · the talent texts quoted on each sheet
(pp. 50–53) · the six Conditions, the −1-per-Condition stacking and Broken
(pp. 24, 40) · Fear tests, the +1-die-per-PC-present rule and the Fear value
table (pp. 67–68) · weapon damage/range/bonus (pp. 76–77) · mementos
healing a Condition (p. 22) · the Advantage at +2 dice, once per mystery
(p. 24) · the six Society offices (p. 85) · Steward Algot Frisk (p. 86) · and
the single-mystery exemption that settles the headquarters question (p. 86).
All six sheets were then re-checked by script against the budgets, caps and
Resources bands: **all six pass.**

**Unverified — flag for the rules pass:**

- `[unverified]` **Mats's Bloodhound on lake ice.** Printed Bloodhound is
  *"+2 to VIGILANCE when tracking your prey."* Extending it to reading rotten
  ice and following what is moving under it is a **GM ruling**, not rules text.
  It is flagged on his sheet and it is the one place this roster asks for a
  ruling rather than quoting one. Decide it in Slot Zero.
- `[unverified]` **Which vaesen the ferryman actually is.** Outline open question
  2 — the office described is closest to a **Näcken** given a psychopomp
  function, but the core bestiary or *Mythic Britain & Ireland* may hold a
  better fit. No sheet depends on the answer: nothing here is built to fight it,
  and the weakness is a name spoken over water, not a stat line. The Fear value
  assumed throughout is **2 (terrible — dead children)**, which is the printed
  band this scenario sits in, but the creature's own Fear value is the
  scenario's to set.
- `[unverified]` **Steward Frisk's motive.** Teddy's Advantage leans on Frisk
  having taken him aside before the sledges left. The core book gives Frisk a
  knowing smile and no explanation (p. 86); what he told Teddy, if anything, is
  a GM option and is written as one.
- `[unverified]` **The 1901 date against Society canon.** The core book sets the
  Society's revival "some ten years ago" relative to its own present; February
  1901 is the outline's date and no sheet contradicts it, but if the house
  timeline for the Society is pinned elsewhere in the repo, Oskar's 1897 job and
  Etta's doctoral year should be checked against it.
