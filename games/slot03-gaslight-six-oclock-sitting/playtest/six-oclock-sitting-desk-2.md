## Headline

**Act Two lands on 2:30 exactly, and it is the best-paced act on the slate.**
The new session clock is internally consistent — all four derived figures
reproduce from the scenario's own 130-fiction-into-75-session ratio — and the
act is built as **fixed beats on a clock with elastic patter between them**,
which absorbs variance without touching the cushion. The 2:30–2:45 cushion went
entirely unspent.

One serious finding, and it is in a fix pass 1 asked for:

1. **THE FLASH — added in v1.1 — cannot happen at the designed table.** The
   photographer is dropped at five players and the scaling has him *"leave at
   the interval"*, which is the break at the end of Act One. At four and five
   players the beat has no addressee, and nothing in Act Two says so. *(text
   hole — critical)*
2. The flash's session figure, **~2:10, sits inside detail two's own beat**
   (2:07 plus four Sanity rolls and Rée's second question). *(timing)*
3. **Two beats carry a bare fiction time and no session figure**, in the one
   scenario whose timing guide says every Act Two beat carries both and to
   *"trust the session figure."* *(text hole)*

### THE FLASH — and the finding that matters

⚠ **At the designed table there is nobody to prompt.** The block says: *"Put the
choice in front of him once, in Miss Carrow's voice"* — *him* being Hoby, the
photographer. The roster's scaling:

> **At five, drop Hoby (6).** *What it costs:* the plates, the flash… *Recovery:*
> the Society's photographer becomes an NPC who sets up, takes his plates and
> **leaves at the interval**.

The interval is the break at the end of Act One. **At five players and at the
four-player designed table, the photographer is out of the house before Act Two
begins.** The v1.1 beat was written for the six-player branch and never
reconciled with the scaling, which already had a different answer for the
missing hard beat (*"use Kite's reaching-rod, produced and laid on the table in
the dark"*).

Two definitions of the same beat now exist in two files, and the one that fires
at the table the scenario calls *designed* is the older one. *(text hole —
critical)*

**The fix is already on the roster and unused.** `Mr Gideon Pell — Journalist`
carries **Art/Craft (Photography) 25**. A journalist who has spent Act One
proving a fraud wants the picture more than the Society's retained man does. At
four and five players, hand Pell the camera and the beat survives intact, in
character, with its choice and its cost unchanged.

## Fix list

### 1. Give the flash an addressee at four and five players *(critical — text hole)*

**Problem.** THE FLASH is addressed to Hoby, who leaves at the interval at five
players and does not exist at four. The designed table cannot run the beat, and
the roster's scaling already answers the gap differently.

**Change.** Add to THE FLASH block:

> **At four or five players the photographer is an NPC and has gone at the
> interval — so the camera is Pell's.** He is a journalist who has spent an hour
> proving a fraud and he has `Art/Craft (Photography) 25` on his sheet; hand him
> the plate-holder as Hoby leaves and let Miss Carrow, who is an NPC at this
> table anyway, ask *him* the question. If Pell is not at the table either, use
> the reaching-rod beat the roster names instead.

**Where.** `scenarios/six-oclock-sitting.md`, THE FLASH block in Act Two.

### 2. Move the flash's session figure clear of detail two *(high — timing)*

**Problem.** ~2:10 falls inside detail two, which measures 2:07 → 2:13.

**Change.** *"session ~2:14, immediately after detail two has been let land"*.

**Where.** Same block, the heading line.

### 3. Give 7:20 its session figure, twice *(medium — text hole)*

**Change.** *"she stops performing entirely at about 7:20 (**session ~2:01**)"*
in the scoring guidance, and *"in one breath at about 7:20 (**session ~2:01**)"*
in cut 2.

**Where.** Same file, THE TRICK LEDGER scoring guidance and Pacing Note cut 2.

### 4. State Act Two's Sanity range *(low)*

**Change.** After detail three: *"**Act Two's whole Sanity cost** is three
checks — 0/1, 1/1D2, and 1/1D3 for the named investigator — so **2–6 points for
the one who is named and 1–4 for everyone else, and no bout of madness is
possible here.** The madness, if any, belongs to Act Three."*

**Where.** Same file, end of *The three true details*.
