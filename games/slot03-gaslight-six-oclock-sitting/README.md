# Slot 3 · Wed 27 Jan, evening — THE SIX O'CLOCK SITTING

*Every trick in this room is a trick. One thing is not, and she doesn't know which.*

**System:** Call of Cthulhu 7e — **Gaslight (1894)** · **4–6 players** · 4-hour slot
(3h30 budget, 3h15 planned) · **one room, one evening** · pure table-talk · no chase, no fight, no
props beyond three handouts.

**Status:** **v1.1 — desk pass 1 applied; desk pass 2 run.** Next: apply pass 2's four fixes &mdash; **THE FLASH has no addressee at four or five players**, which is the critical one &mdash; then pass 3 from the player chair **at six**, which nothing has tested.

**Remaining prep:** handouts (the Society's exposure
checklist, the sitter's notebook, one unsent letter) · pass-2 fixes, then desk pass 3 · print
pack. **No art budget, no map, no set-piece page** — the cheapest game on the
slate to produce, deliberately.

**Era note:** written for Gaslight rather than the 1920s on purpose. The Society
for Psychical Research was founded in 1882 and the great era of cabinet mediums
and systematic exposure *is* the 1880s and 90s — the scenario is more at home
here than it was in 1923, and it costs nothing to move.
