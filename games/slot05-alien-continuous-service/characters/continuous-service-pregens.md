# CONTINUOUS SERVICE — Pre-Generated Characters (6 sheets, plays at 4–6)

**Status: v1.1.** Six sheets, written, **plus the two survivor seats' agenda
cards** (added at desk pass 1, 2026-11-03 — they were the scenario's one
blocking gap). ALIEN RPG — **Cinematic play**.

The crew of the salvage tug **USCSS *Hawser***, boarding the ore hauler
*Keelson* in high orbit over LV-871, 2186.

Three goals:

1. **Rules-accurate.** Every sheet audits against the ALIEN RPG core rulebook,
   ch. 2 (pp. 26–33) and ch. 4 (pp. 74–79). Attribute and skill budgets, Health
   derivation, the one-career-talent limit and the skill cap on non-career
   skills are shown inline so the maths is checkable. See **Build budget** below.
2. **A working party.** Six lanes with deliberately low overlap: command
   (Oyelaran), cutting and machinery (Agbaje), flight and systems (Halabi),
   bodies and mercy (Pruitt), records and the ledger (Marchetti), eyes and the
   law (Cobb). Nobody sits skill-dead on either Act Two track.
3. **Wired into the case.** This is Cinematic ALIEN, so the agendas are not
   decoration — they are the scenario's engine. **Each sheet carries three
   agendas, one per Act**, as the core rules require (p. 31). They are written
   to collide. The collision map is at the end of this file and the GM should
   read it before Act One.

> **Errors fixed from previous versions:** none — this is v1, built from the
> primed brief. The brief is superseded by this file.
>
> **Deliberate deviation from the outline, flagged for review:** the outline's
> design sketch was five seats and said "at four, drop the tug's medic and give
> her agenda to the engineer." This roster is six, and the **Pilot** drops
> first, then the Medic (whose agendas still go to the Roughneck/engineer, as
> the outline directs). Reason: the Officer's ICC Commercial Flight Officer
> licence and Piloting 1 make the Pilot the only genuinely recoverable lane, and
> the sixth seat — a **Colonial Marshal** — exists because Act Three is a
> signature. Two pens with two competing authorities in the room is the climax.
> Say the word and I'll cut the Marshal and restore the five-seat sketch.

## Build budget (the numbers every audit line below refers to)

The core rulebook gives **one** creation budget (p. 28):

- **14 attribute points** across STRENGTH, AGILITY, WITS, EMPATHY. No attribute
  below 2 and none above 4 — **except** the career's **key attribute**, which
  may be 5.
- **10 skill points.** Up to **3** in each of your career's three key skills.
  **A single point only** in any other skill.
- **One career talent**, chosen from the three your career offers.
- **Health = your STRENGTH score** (p. 30). **Stress Level starts at 0** (p. 30)
  unless the scenario says otherwise.
- **Signature item** (p. 33): a tiny, sentimentally valuable thing. Its rule is
  the house standard's "mechanical hook" already baked in — **once per Act in
  Cinematic play you may interact with it to reduce your Stress Level by one
  step; this usually costs a slow action** (p. 104).
- **Cinematic gear is set by the scenario**, not by the career gear list
  (p. 33). The kit below is therefore a GM choice, chosen from each career's
  list so it stays recognisable; swap freely.

**There is no separate Cinematic build budget in the core rulebook.** Every
sheet below is audited against the budget above. If a later rules pass finds a
Cinematic-specific budget in a supplement, re-audit — but do not assume one
exists.

**Core roll, for the stand-in GM:** attribute + skill = that many Base Dice,
plus one Stress Die per point of Stress. One or more **6** is a success.
A failed roll may be **pushed** once (reroll everything, +1 Stress) — but if any
Stress Die shows the 💀, you cannot push and must make a **Panic Roll**
(D6 + current Stress Level, table p. 104). Stress Level drops one point per full
Turn resting somewhere safe.

## 1. Captain DESSA OYELARAN — Officer · master of the *Hawser*

*She holds the contract, the debt and the only authority on this tug that anyone has ever obeyed.*

| | | | |
|---|---|---|---|
| **STR 3** | **AGI 2** | **WITS 4** | **EMP 5** *(key)* |

**Health 3** *(= STRENGTH)* · **Stress 0**

**Skills:** Command 3 · Manipulation 3 · Ranged Combat 1 · Comtech 1 ·
Observation 1 · Piloting 1 — all others 0
*(Budget: 3+2+4+5 = 14 attr ✓, EMP 5 permitted as Officer key attribute ✓;
3+3+1+1+1+1 = 10 skill ✓; Command, Manipulation and Ranged Combat are the
Officer's key skills so may exceed 1 ✓; Comtech/Observation/Piloting at 1 each ✓)*

**Talent — PULL RANK:** use **Command** to order other non-Officer PCs and NPCs
in your organisation to do a specific thing. Roll Command against the target's
Manipulation. On a success they must obey, **even if it harms them**. Your Stress
Level increases by one each time. One roll covers one action, and it cannot stop
a panic action.

**Gear:** M4A3 Service Pistol (Bonus +2, Damage 1, Medium range, ½ weight, two
reloads) · Seegson P-DAT (coordinate info for field teams — this is the thing
the *Keelson*'s deck plans get loaded onto)
**Signature item:** Her **ICC Commercial Flight Officer licence**, laminated,
twenty-two years old, the photograph of a woman she does not recognise.
*(Once per Act: look at it, reduce Stress one step. Slow action.)*

**Buddy:** Agbaje. **Rival:** Cobb.

**Key memory:** Signing for the *Hawser* at a Lasalle Bionational desk on
Gateway, aged thirty-one, with a pen somebody else had warmed. The clerk said
"congratulations, captain" in the voice people use for a diagnosis.
**Key relationship:** **Ruben Agbaje**, her deck boss, aboard longer than the
charter. He has never once asked her for anything, which she has decided to read
as loyalty rather than as the other thing.

**Private (do not share):** *The Hawser's charter payment is forty days overdue.
Lasalle does not repossess tugs; it sells the crew's indentures on and lets
somebody else sort out the hull. You have not told them. You have told yourself
that telling them would not help, which is true, and that is not why you haven't.*

*The job pays on a towed hull, not on a report. You have known since you read the
contract that there is a version of this night where the right thing to do and
the thing that keeps six people employed are not the same sentence, and you have
been quietly betting it would not come up.*

**AGENDAS** *(hidden; one handed out per Act)*

- **Act One:** *Get the tow rig on that hull before anyone finds a reason not to.*
  Every minute spent reading is a minute not rigging. Push them.
- **Act Two:** *Whatever is true about this ship, it does not leave it as a
  report.* No transmission, no log entry, no witness statement, until the hull
  is rigged and the *Hawser* is under way.
- **Act Three:** *Be the one who signs.* Not out of courage. A signature in your
  own hand is a signature you can explain afterwards, and somebody else's is a
  thing that gets explained to you.

**Playing Dessa:** give the order once, plainly, then wait — you do not repeat
yourself · you touch people on the upper arm when you need them to move · you
answer a question about the contract with a question about the schedule.

## 2. RUBEN AGBAJE — Roughneck · deck boss and rigger

*Five lifts of a man, thirty years of other people's holds, and one rule he will not bend tonight.*

| | | | |
|---|---|---|---|
| **STR 5** *(key)* | **AGI 3** | **WITS 3** | **EMP 3** |

**Health 5** *(= STRENGTH — the most survivable sheet on the table)* · **Stress 0**

**Skills:** Heavy Machinery 3 · Stamina 3 · Close Combat 2 · Comtech 1 ·
Observation 1 — all others 0
*(Budget: 5+3+3+3 = 14 attr ✓, STR 5 permitted as Roughneck key attribute ✓;
3+3+2+1+1 = 10 skill ✓; Heavy Machinery, Stamina and Close Combat are the
Roughneck's key skills ✓)*

**Talent — THE LONG HAUL:** nothing surprises you any more. **Once per Act in
Cinematic play, you may ignore all 💀 from a single roll.** (Which means: you
push that roll anyway, and you do not panic.)

**Gear:** Cutting Torch (**as a tool: Heavy Machinery +2**; **as a weapon: Damage
3, Engaged range, armour piercing**; Power Supply 5, roll after every use) ·
IRC Mk.50 Compression Suit (Armour Rating 2, Air Supply 5, **AGILITY −1**)
**Signature item:** A short length of **8mm wire rope**, frayed at one end,
spliced at the other, carried in a hip pocket for eleven years.
*(Once per Act: work the splice with your thumb, reduce Stress one step.)*

**Buddy:** Pruitt. **Rival:** Halabi.

**Key memory:** The *Corvina*, eleven years ago. A man called Esteban Oyola went
into a hold because Ruben said the atmosphere was fine, and the atmosphere was
fine, and the hold was not. Ruben carried him out in two trips.
**Key relationship:** **Captain Oyelaran.** He has never asked her for a raise,
a berth or a favour, because he worked out early that she would give him any of
them and then be short of it herself.

**Private (do not share):** *You do not send people into holds. Not "you prefer
not to" — you do not. You go first and you come out last, and in thirty years
nobody has had to be told this twice.*

*The part you have never said: it is not courage and it is not penance. It is
that you cannot be in a corridor waiting. You found that out on the Corvina, in
the four minutes between sending Esteban in and going in after him, and you
have organised your entire working life around never finding it out again.*

**AGENDAS** *(hidden; one handed out per Act)*

- **Act One:** *You cut the lock and you go through it first.* Be the first
  aboard and the last off the deck, every time, whatever it costs the schedule.
- **Act Two:** *Somebody on this ship is still breathing. Find them yourself.*
  Not on a terminal — with your hands, down whichever passage it takes.
- **Act Three:** *Nobody is aboard the Keelson when the tow starts.* Not the
  purser. Not the deck chief. Not one of yours. If the answer requires a person
  to stay, the answer is wrong.

**Playing Ruben:** put your hand flat on a bulkhead before you trust it · you
speak in the imperative and only about work · when somebody says "we'll have to
leave them," look at them until they say something else.

## 3. NOOR HALABI — Pilot · the *Hawser*'s hands

*She has not been last out of anywhere since she was twenty-four, and she counts the minutes to the lock like a pulse.*

| | | | |
|---|---|---|---|
| **STR 2** | **AGI 5** *(key)* | **WITS 4** | **EMP 3** |

**Health 2** *(= STRENGTH — two hits and she is Broken; she knows it)* · **Stress 0**

**Skills:** Piloting 3 · Comtech 3 · Ranged Combat 1 · Observation 1 ·
Mobility 1 · Survival 1 — all others 0
*(Budget: 2+5+4+3 = 14 attr ✓, AGI 5 permitted as Pilot key attribute ✓;
3+3+1+1+1+1 = 10 skill ✓; Piloting, Ranged Combat and Comtech are the Pilot's
key skills, so Comtech may go to 3 ✓)*

**Talent — RECKLESS:** you push harder than others dare. **You can push any
skill roll based on AGILITY twice**, not just once. Each push increases your
Stress Level by one.
*(Alternative on the same career, if the GM prefers a tighter sheet: **Like the
Back of Your Hand** — +2 Piloting with one specific named craft, i.e. the
Hawser.)*

**Gear:** **PR-PUT Uplink Terminal** (remote control of a spacecraft, on a
Comtech roll — she can fly the *Hawser* from inside the *Keelson*; this item is
the scenario's loaded gun) · IRC Mk.50 Compression Suit (Armour 2, Air Supply 5,
AGILITY −1)
**Signature item:** Her **pilot's logbook**, with nine years of hours in it and
one page torn out.
*(Once per Act: read an old entry, reduce Stress one step.)*

**Buddy:** Marchetti. **Rival:** Agbaje.

**Key memory:** The *Sheaf*, aged twenty-four. She undocked on the stated hour
with three people still inboard, because the stated hour was the stated hour,
and she has flown tugs instead of liners ever since because a tug's crew is
small enough to count from the cockpit.
**Key relationship:** **Celia Marchetti.** The Company woman is the only person
aboard who has never once implied that Noor should have waited, and Noor has
not worked out whether that is kindness or arithmetic.

**Private (do not share):** *Three people. You know their names. You have never
said them aloud on this ship and you are not going to.*

*What you tell yourself is that the hour was the hour. What is actually true is
that you were twenty-four and frightened and you moved your hand, and that every
procedure you have followed with such beautiful precision since has been a
structure built around that one movement so that it never has to be a choice
again. Tonight somebody is going to ask you to make it a choice.*

**AGENDAS** *(hidden; one handed out per Act)*

- **Act One:** *Never be the last one aboard.* Know, at every moment, how many
  minutes you are from the *Hawser*, and make sure at least one route back is
  open and yours.
- **Act Two:** *Get the uplink live from inside this hull.* If the terminal
  talks to the *Hawser*, you never have to walk back. Do it quietly.
- **Act Three:** *The Hawser undocks on the hour written in the contract.* With
  or without the rest of them. You have done this before. You know you can.

**Playing Noor:** state the time out loud, unprompted, roughly every ten minutes
· you stand nearest whichever door you came in by · you agree with plans
pleasantly and then check your own line of retreat.

## 4. WEN PRUITT — Medic · ship's medical officer

*Counts the bodies herself. Has signed a list that was wrong, once, and will not do it again.*

| | | | |
|---|---|---|---|
| **STR 2** | **AGI 3** | **WITS 4** | **EMP 5** *(key)* |

**Health 2** *(= STRENGTH)* · **Stress 0**

**Skills:** Medical Aid 3 · Observation 3 · Mobility 1 · Comtech 1 ·
Manipulation 1 · Survival 1 — all others 0
*(Budget: 2+3+4+5 = 14 attr ✓, EMP 5 permitted as Medic key attribute ✓;
3+3+1+1+1+1 = 10 skill ✓; Mobility, Observation and Medical Aid are the Medic's

**Talent — FIELD SURGEON:** **+2 to MEDICAL AID when treating someone who is

*(Alternative on the same career, and the better pick at a four-player table

**Gear:** Personal Medkit (**MEDICAL AID +2**) · Surgical Kit (**MEDICAL AID +1

> **The number to know, and the one scene it is for.** Banks. Medical Aid 3 +
> EMPATHY 5 = **8 Base Dice**, +2 Field Surgeon, +2 Personal Medkit = **12
> dice**. *Note the core restriction: you may only use the gear bonus from one
> piece of gear on a roll (core rulebook, "Gear" ch. 3)* — so the Surgical Kit's
> +1 does not stack with the Medkit's +2; pick the medkit. **She will not fail
> that roll.** Which is the point: the scene is not whether Aurelio Banks can be
> kept alive. It is whether he should be.

**Signature item:** Her **last psych evaluation**, folded into eighths: *"All
clear at last."*
*(Once per Act: unfold it, reduce Stress one step.)*

**Buddy:** Cobb. **Rival:** Marchetti.

**Key memory:** The *Antwerp*'s casualty list, which she compiled, checked and
signed. It was wrong by two. Both of them turned up alive in a Thedus clinic
nine weeks later, and nobody was angry with her, and that was worse.
**Key relationship:** **Deputy Marshal Cobb.** They have a running agreement,
never stated, that he writes nothing she has not seen and she says nothing he
cannot write down.

**Private (do not share):** *You know exactly what you are. You are not the
person who saves people. You are the person who gets the list right. That is a
smaller thing and it is the thing you are actually good at, and you have spent
four years deciding whether that is a confession or a job description.*

*And underneath it: when you say "end the suffering," you have never once been
certain which of the two things you mean, and you have never had to be.*

**AGENDAS** *(hidden; one handed out per Act)*

- **Act One:** *Count the bodies yourself.* You do not accept a number off a
  terminal, a manifest or a roster. Thirty-eight, forty-one, whatever it says —
  you want to have seen them.
- **Act Two:** *If anything on this ship is suffering, end the suffering.*
  Whatever "end" turns out to mean. You will know when you are looking at it.
- **Act Three:** *No living person is entered as dead, and no dead person is
  entered as living.* The list is the list. You will not sign a wrong one twice.

**Playing Wen:** say the number out loud every time it changes · you touch a
body before you talk about it · ask "what are you going to write?" before you
ask anything else.

## 5. CELIA MARCHETTI — Company Agent · Weyland-Yutani, Liabilities

*Not here for the hull. Here because a ledger in Gateway has a line on it that does not add up, and she is the line's auditor.*

| | | | |
|---|---|---|---|
| **STR 2** | **AGI 3** | **WITS 5** *(key)* | **EMP 4** |

**Health 2** *(= STRENGTH)* · **Stress 0**

**Skills:** Comtech 3 · Manipulation 3 · Observation 1 · Command 1 ·
Piloting 1 · Medical Aid 1 — all others 0
*(Budget: 2+3+5+4 = 14 attr ✓, WITS 5 permitted as Company Agent key attribute ✓;
3+3+1+1+1+1 = 10 skill ✓; Comtech, Observation and Manipulation are the Agent's
key skills, so Comtech and Manipulation may go to 3; **Observation deliberately
held at 1** so she does not tread on Cobb's lane ✓)*

**Talent — TAKE CONTROL:** **you roll MANIPULATION using WITS instead of
EMPATHY.** With WITS 5 + Manipulation 3 that is **8 Base Dice** — the most
persuasive sheet on the tug, and none of it is warmth.
*(Alternative, if the table wants teeth: **Personal Safety** — when you are
attacked or in fatal danger and another PC or friendly NPC is within Short
range, roll Manipulation; on a success the other character suffers the attack or
hazard instead of you. +1 Stress. It is the single nastiest button in the core
book and this scenario can absolutely hold it.)*

**Gear:** **Data transmitter card with corporate clearance level** (this is what
closes a payroll) · **Gold-plated pen**
> Those two objects are Act Three. Put them on the table in front of her player
> in Act One and never mention them again until it matters.

**Signature item:** Her **letter of corporate authorisation**, signed by a
Vice-President of Liabilities whom she has never met.
*(Once per Act: reread the second paragraph, reduce Stress one step.)*

**Buddy:** Oyelaran. **Rival:** Cobb.

**Key memory:** Her first audit, eight years ago: a mining concern on Thedus
where the discrepancy turned out to be fourteen men who had been dead for a
quarter and whose supervisor had kept drawing their pay. She found it in a
morning. She was praised. She has looked for that feeling since.
**Key relationship:** **Noor Halabi.** The pilot is the only person on this tug
who understands that a stated hour is a stated hour, and Celia has filed that
under *useful* and tries not to think about the other word.

**Private (do not share):** *Nine containers were loaded at a contractor's
waypoint in 2175. The Company's copy of the manifest lists eight. That is why
you are aboard, and nobody — not the captain, not Liabilities, not the
underwriter — has been told that the discrepancy is in the consignment and not
in the payroll.*

*Here is the thing you have never allowed yourself to finish thinking. You are
very good at this. The reason you are good at it is that you can look at
fourteen dead men on a ledger and see the ledger. You have never once wondered
whether that is the skill or the damage.*

**AGENDAS** *(hidden; one handed out per Act)*

- **Act One:** *Find the ninth entry.* Nine containers went aboard. The
  Company's manifest lists eight. Get into the cargo record and find out what
  the ninth one was, without explaining to anybody why you are looking.
- **Act Two:** *Establish on record that the consignment is intact and
  recoverable.* Nobody vents it, burns it or jettisons it. Whatever else happens
  tonight, those containers stay where they are.
- **Act Three:** *Close the payroll.* Eleven years of accruing wages is a
  liability, and a liability closes one of two ways: forty-one death filings, or
  a sale that makes the whole thing somebody else's asset. Either is a win. The
  *Keelson* going into LV-871's atmosphere is not.

**Playing Celia:** write down what people say while they are still saying it ·
agree with the last thing somebody said before disagreeing with all of it · when
asked a direct question, answer a slightly different one, pleasantly.

*(Once per Act: close your hand round it, reduce Stress one step.)*

**Buddy:** Pruitt. **Rival:** Marchetti.

**Key memory:** His father wrote a death certificate for a man who walked into
the office four months later, and spent the rest of his service being the

**Key relationship:** **Wen Pruitt.** The medic is the only person on this

**Private (do not share):** *You are forty blanks short and you have known since
Gateway. You could have requisitioned more. You did not, because some part of
you wanted to arrive aboard this ship already unable to finish the job, so that
whatever you decided would look like circumstance.*

*You have been a deputy for nineteen years and you have never once been the
senior officer in a room where the decision mattered. Tonight you are. You have
wanted that and dreaded it in exactly equal measure for nineteen years, and
there is nobody above you out here to send it to.*

**AGENDAS** *(hidden; one handed out per Act)*

- **Act One:** *Establish that this is a derelict — or do not.* Your signature is

  out what you are actually looking at before you do. Do not let the captain
  rush you.
- **Act Two:** *Find out who has been filing the timesheets, and whether it is a

  eleven years. Somebody is a different legal problem from something.
- **Act Three:** *Forty-one names get a certificate with a date on it, or you do
  not leave.* You are forty blanks short. Work out what that means before anyone
  asks you.

**Playing Harlan:** stand in a doorway and look at the whole room before you
enter it · ask for a name and a date, every time, and write both down · you do
not raise the shotgun; you hold it like a tool you are carrying somewhere.

## Agenda collision map (GM reference — read before Act One)

*The scenario's open question "does the collision at 2:00 need written pairs?"
was closed at desk pass 1 against this section: it does, and they are here.*

The agendas are the scenario. This is what they do to each other.

| Act | Collision | What it looks like at the table |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Oyelaran ↔ Cobb** | She needs the hull rigged now; his signature is what makes the salvage lawful and he is deliberately slow. She has **Pull Rank** (Command 8 vs his Manipulation 4) and he is not in her organisation, so it does not work on him — *check that at the table; it is the first thing she will try.* |
| **1** | **Pruitt ↔ Oyelaran** | "Count the bodies yourself" versus "stop reading and start rigging." Low-stakes version of Act Three, played early so the table learns the shape. |
| **1** | **Marchetti ↔ everyone** | She is looking for a cargo entry and will not say so. Cobb's **Investigator** is the single most likely thing to catch her doing it. |
| **2** | **Agbaje ↔ Halabi** | He is going down a passage to find whoever is breathing. She is getting the uplink live so she never has to walk back. They are Rivals on the sheet and this is why. |
| **2** | **Marchetti ↔ Halabi** | Seven sealed containers are mass and hours the *Hawser* does not have. Her Buddy becomes her problem. |
| **2** | **Cobb ↔ Oyelaran** | He is trying to establish *who* is filing. She has told herself nothing leaves this ship as a report. One of them is going to have to lie out loud. |
| **2** | **Pruitt ↔ Agbaje** | Both are heading for Banks. He intends to carry him out. She may intend something else. Neither has said which. |
| **3** | **Cobb ↔ Marchetti** | **The centre of the scenario.** Two pens, two authorities, one ledger. A Form 11-C closes forty-one lives; a clearance card closes a liability. They are each other's Rival and only one signature gets filed. |
| **3** | **Agbaje ↔ Halabi** | Hard, unresolvable, and the table will feel it coming for ninety minutes. The *Hawser* undocks on the hour. Nobody stays aboard. Both cannot be true. |
| **3** | **Pruitt ↔ Cobb and Marchetti** | The **Filed** ending requires two living people — Vos and Banks — to appear on a death list. Wen will not sign a wrong list twice. She is the friction on the cleanest ending. |
| **3** | **Marchetti ↔ everyone** | **Burned** is the only ending she cannot accept, and it is the one a frightened table reaches for. If someone says "we put her into the atmosphere," she has Manipulation 8 and a reason. |

**Achievability check (3h30).** Every agenda above resolves inside the act it
belongs to, with one object or one sentence: a torch cut, a terminal read, a
door opened, a name spoken, a form signed. None requires a subsystem, a chase or
a rules lecture. **Read each agenda aloud to its player as a handout at the top
of its act** (core rulebook p. 31) and award a Story Point at the end of the act
for pursuing it at real cost — **and do not say why** (p. 213).

**Story Points:** spend one for an automatic 6, before or after a roll; maximum
three held at once (p. 61).

## When your character dies

ALIEN is lethal, this scenario is lethal, and the safety line says so in the
first thirty seconds. **High lethality is expected.** Here is the written
answer, so no player sits out:

1. **You keep your Story Points.** Verified rule: *"Story Points belong to
   players, not PCs"* (core rulebook, Epilogue, p. 383). They carry straight
   across to your new character.
2. **You take one of the *Keelson*'s two survivors** — **Purser Ileana Vos** or
   **Deck Chief Aurelio Banks** — both of whom are written, and both of whom
   know things your dead character never did. First death takes Vos; second
   takes Banks. (At four players, the survivor seats matter more, not less —
   they are how a thinned table gets back to five mouths in the room.)
3. **The GM hands you that survivor's agenda for the current Act**, in the same
   format as the sheets above, within a minute — **the cards are written, below,
   under *The Keelson's two survivors*.** You do not get a briefing; you get a
   card and the floor.
4. **If a PC turns on the party**, the core rule applies: *"play the current
   scene to an end, and then turn the PC into an NPC"* (p. 213). Then the player
   takes a survivor as above. PvP is on the table here and it has a written exit.
5. **Both survivor seats carry their own three agendas** — below. The incoming
   player gets a real card, not the dead PC's card re-pointed.

**If the second death happens in Act One**, before anybody has been in the
crawls, the player still takes Banks: they start as **a voice above the cargo
spine that the rest of the table has not met**, they know exactly where they
are, and they cannot be seen. Play the first scene from inside the crawl. It is
the best version of this and it costs nothing.

## The *Keelson*'s two survivors — the death-replacement seats

**These are NPC lines, not built PCs.** They are deliberately not audited
against the character-creation budget above, because ALIEN does not build NPCs
that way and because a player taking one of these seats mid-session needs four
numbers and three sentences, not a sheet. Hand over the Act in progress.

---

### VOS — Purser ILEANA VOS · *Keelson*, eleven years aboard

*She has clocked forty-one dead people in every ninety days for eleven years,
and she is not mad, and that is the problem.*

| | | | |
|---|---|---|---|
| **STR 2** | **AGI 2** | **WITS 4** *(key)* | **EMP 3** |

**Health 2** · **Stress 3** *(she starts stressed; she has been alone with this
since 2175)*

**Skills:** Comtech 3 · Observation 2 · Manipulation 2 · Survival 1 —
all others 0

**Gear:** the purser's office key, which is the only one · a hand terminal with
eleven years of wake logs on it · **the forty-one timesheets, carried**

**What she knows that your dead character did not:**
- The payroll is not fraud. It is the **only thing keeping the ship crewed**,
  and a crewed ship cannot be declared derelict and towed.
- A tow ends at a dock with eleven thousand people on it.
- **Containers 1 and 4 were opened aboard, on the ninth day, by someone with
  cargo authority.** She knows who. She has never written it down.
- Banks is alive. She has never opened her door to him and she will tell you so
  without being asked.

**AGENDAS** *(hidden; one per Act, handed over when you take the seat)*

- **Act One:** *Keep the roster alive.* Nobody gets logged off. Not by a
  salvage crew, not by a form, not by accident. Whatever is said in the next
  hour, the forty-one stay on shift.
- **Act Two:** *Nobody opens the holds.* Seven seals, and the two that are
  already broken are the whole history of this ship. Argue, mislead, lock,
  lie — but do not let them cut a seal while you are in the room.
- **Act Three:** *Find the one who will take the cycle.* You cannot do another
  eleven years. Somebody at this table can be made to understand it, and the
  ones who understand it are not the ones who volunteer. **Work out which of
  them it is, and ask them.**

**Playing Ileana:** answer the question that was asked and no more · you are
courteous, not warm · you have had eleven years to assemble every argument and
you deploy them in order · when somebody says *"you could just have left"*,
agree with them.

### BANKS — Deck Chief AURELIO BANKS · eleven months in a maintenance crawl

*He wants off, he is not wrong about anything, and he agrees with everybody.*

| | | | |
|---|---|---|---|
| **STR 2** *(was 4)* | **AGI 2** | **WITS 3** | **EMP 4** *(key)* |

**Health 2** · **Stress 4** *(the highest on the table, and he is cheerful)*

**Skills:** Heavy Machinery 2 · Survival 3 · Observation 2 · Comtech 1 —
all others 0
*(He was a deck chief. **Eleven months in a crawl has taken his strength and
his legs**: he cannot run, he cannot climb a ladder unassisted, and the first
time he stands upright somebody has to hold him. Do not play this as frailty —
play it as a man who has budgeted around it.)*

**Gear:** a condensation line, a torch with a dying cell, **a deck chief's
master key for every hatch on the cargo spine**

**What he knows that your dead character did not:**
- Where all nine containers went, and that **the thing came out of cradle 4**.
- That two were opened aboard, and roughly when.
- That the crawls are how it moves, which is how he is still alive: he has
  learned its route and he sleeps off it.
- That Vos did right. He says this unprompted and he means it.

**AGENDAS** *(hidden; one per Act)*

- **Act One:** *Get off this ship.* That is all. You have been patient for
  eleven months and the patience is finished. Say yes to anything that ends
  with you on another hull.
- **Act Two:** *Be useful enough to be worth taking.* They have no reason to
  carry a man who cannot walk. Give them one, every few minutes, and keep
  giving them one. **Tell them the truth — it is the only currency you have
  and it happens to be real.**
- **Act Three:** *Do not let them leave Vos behind to do it alone.* You have
  spent a year not blaming her for a closed door. You are not going to spend
  the rest of your life having walked away from the same one.

**Playing Aurelio:** talk — too much, fast, and gratefully · agree with the
last person who spoke · never ask for anything directly · the one thing you
will not do is say a word against Ileana Vos.

## Player-count scaling

Six sheets exist. **The game plays at four.**

**Drop order: the Pilot (Halabi) first, then the Medic (Pruitt).**

### At five — drop HALABI (Pilot)

- **Why her first:** she is the only genuinely recoverable lane. **Oyelaran has
  Piloting 1 (AGI 2 = 3 dice) and the ICC Commercial Flight Officer licence as
  her signature item**, which is the written in-fiction cover; **Marchetti also
  has Piloting 1**. The *Hawser* is docked, not manoeuvring.
- **What it costs:** the **PR-PUT Uplink Terminal** leaves the table, so there
  is no remote-flight option and the tug must be physically walked back to. This
  makes the game *tighter*, not looser — Act Three's clock becomes a corridor
  rather than a keystroke. If the GM wants the option kept, move the uplink
  terminal onto Oyelaran's gear line and her Act Three agenda becomes: *the
  Hawser undocks on the hour, and you are the one holding the terminal.*
- **Her Act Three agenda moves to Oyelaran** and replaces her Act Three agenda
  entirely: *"Be the one who signs"* becomes *"Be the one who signs, and undock
  on the hour, and those are the same decision."* Stronger, if anything.
- **Buddy/Rival repairs:** Agbaje's Rival becomes **Marchetti**. Everyone else
  is unchanged.

### At four — drop HALABI and PRUITT (Pilot and Medic)

- **Four sheets left:** Oyelaran, Agbaje, Marchetti, Cobb. Lanes: command and
  people / cutting and machinery / records and the ledger / eyes and the law.
  Keys: EMP, STR, WITS, WITS.
- **Pruitt's agendas move to Agbaje**, as the outline directs. They sit on him
  without friction — *count the bodies yourself* is already how he works, *end
  the suffering* is Banks, and *no living person entered as dead* is one sentence
  from *nobody is aboard when the tow starts*. Give him **both** Act Three
  agendas and let him feel the weight.
- **What it costs, stated plainly:** Banks' scene loses its specialist.
  **Medical Aid drops to 1 across the table** (Marchetti and Cobb, 5 and 4 dice
  respectively). **Move the Personal Medkit (+2) onto Agbaje's gear line.** And
  then do not make the scene a roll — it was never supposed to be one. Wen's
  twelve dice existed to prove that *keeping Banks alive is easy*; with her gone,
  say so out loud instead. The scene is the decision, and the decision does not
  need Medical Aid 3.
- **Also lost:** the softest voice in the room. With Pruitt gone there is nobody
  between Marchetti and Cobb in Act Three. **Expect the ending to come faster
  and harder at four.** That is acceptable; note it in the playtest.
- **Buddy/Rival repairs:** Agbaje — Buddy **Oyelaran**, Rival **Marchetti**.
  Cobb — Buddy **Agbaje**, Rival **Marchetti** (unchanged). Oyelaran and
  Marchetti unchanged.

## Split recommendations (nudge, don't force)

**Act One: do not split.** The walk is the whole act and it wants six torches in
one corridor. Give Cobb the rooms (**Investigator**, one roll per Turn per
location), Agbaje the locks and the deck, Marchetti the terminals, Pruitt the
bunks and the galley, Halabi the ship's systems and the way back, Oyelaran the
schedule she is losing.

**Act Two splits into Track A (the purser's office — Vos) and Track B (the cargo
spine — Banks and the containers).** Alternate in ~12-minute segments, each
ending on a pull.

| Table | Track A — the purser's office | Track B — the cargo spine |
|---|---|---|
| **Six** | Oyelaran · Marchetti · Cobb — the three authorities, which is where Vos's argument lands hardest | Agbaje · Pruitt · Halabi |
| **Five** | Oyelaran · Marchetti · Cobb | Agbaje · Pruitt |
| **Four** | Oyelaran · Marchetti | Agbaje · Cobb |

**How a 2+2 becomes a 2+1** — a player steps out, arrives late, or dies and is
between characters:

> **The solo side is always Track A, the purser's office. Never the cargo
> spine.** The office is a conversation with a sixty-one-year-old woman behind a
> pressure door; a lone PC there is tense and survivable. A lone PC in the cargo
> spine is a corridor with one torch in it and a creature in the scenario, and
> that is not a split, it is an execution. If the table insists on a solo in the
> spine, make the pull at the end of the segment a reason to come back.

At **four**, nobody is idle during Act Two, so **the GM runs both tracks without
a sidelined pair**. If the table is five or six and a pair is waiting, give them
the handouts: the wake logs are forty minutes repeated forty-five times and
reading them *is* a job.
