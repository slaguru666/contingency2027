# Slot 5 · Thu 28 Jan, afternoon — THE SKY RIDE

*Eleven people have gone missing this season. The Fair has nine days left and it is not closing.*

**System:** Pulp Cthulhu (CoC 7e) · **5 players** · 4-hour slot (3h30 play) ·
**the slate's one straight monster hunt** · a city-block map, a crowd, a deadline
· climax on a cable car two hundred feet above the lagoon.
