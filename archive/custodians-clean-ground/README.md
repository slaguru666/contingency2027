# Slot 9 · Sun 31 Jan, afternoon — CLEAN GROUND

*In 1962 the department filed a spare valley, in case it was needed. It was needed.*

**System:** The Custodians (RingBRP) · **4–6 players** · 4-hour slot (4h00 including
a break) · British bureaucratic horror · a cancelled contingency · forty-one people
walking north across a place that has no north.

## Where the scenario lives

**Not in this repo.** CLEAN GROUND is a RingBRP scenario and RingBRP scenarios live
inside the system, where the build can check them:

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `~/FoundryVTT/Data/systems/ringbrp/docs/scenarios/CLEAN_GROUND.md` | The scenario. Self-contained: a GM with this file and the compendium can run it |
| `…/docs/scenarios/CLEAN_GROUND_HANDOUTS.html` | Print-ready pack — four A4 sheets, 11pt floor |
| `…/docs/scenarios/CLEAN_GROUND_PLAYTEST_1–3.md` | Three desk passes: GM chair, Act Four stress, player chair |

That is deliberate rather than untidy. Seventeen guards in the RingBRP build check
this scenario on every run — that every skill it names is rollable by the declared
cast, that its measured figures still match their recorded baselines, that the two
almanac handouts cannot be styled apart. A copy in this repo would be a second
version nothing checks. **Read it there; do not fork it here.**

## Why this slot

Sunday afternoon was held as the re-run reserve. This spends it, which is a real
cost: `docs/schedule.md` says *"if anything on the slate has to give, it is a Sunday
reserve, never a playtest."* That sentence still governs. **If prep on the eight
booked games slips, this is the game that gives** — it is the newest, the least
tested, and the only one whose absence costs nobody a booked seat.

Afternoon rather than evening because it needs 4–6 players for a full four hours,
and Sunday evening is the slot the schedule expects may never fill.

## Two things a GM must know before running it

1. **The lethal encounter is not the monster.** The understudy in Act Four cannot
   hurt anybody; the refugee column in Act Two can wipe the party. EXPOSURE in the
   scenario carries the measured figures.
2. **One pregen cannot fight, on purpose**, and his player must be told before the
   game. His entry says why and what to give him instead.
