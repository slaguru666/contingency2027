Wyrd Sisters Games Club presents

Contingency 2027

27–31 January 2027 · Searles Leisure Resort, Hunstanton · five days of gaming

Twelve games, run by Tim Evans. Four-hour slots, pre-generated characters, no system knowledge needed, safety tools at every table.

Wed · Morning · 5 players

The London Particular

Cthulhu by Gaslight — 1896

Four nights of fog, four deaths, nothing connecting them — until somebody fetches the Meteorological Office’s fog register. Each died exactly where the fog lay thickest. Tonight’s is the worst of the winter, and it will not lift before dawn.

Wed · Afternoon · 4 players

Afterimage

Blade Runner RPG

Los Angeles, 2037. A memory designer is dead and Rep-Detect has the case with a whisper attached: rogue replicant. The whisper comes from a corporation, and it is a lie of the useful kind.

Wed · Evening · 4 players

The Six O’Clock Sitting

Cthulhu by Gaslight — 1894

Mrs Veale will not be difficult, and she isn’t: by seven you have the cabinet, the thread and the whole shabby machine. What you do not have is how she knew the contents of a letter that was never posted.

Thu · Morning · 5 players

Black Summer: Last Flight Out

The Walking Dead Universe

New York, six weeks after the fall. The last plane leaves a Brooklyn airfield at dawn and boarding needs a stamped chit with your face on it. Between the five of you, you have three.

Thu · Afternoon · 6 players

Closing Weekend

Call of Cthulhu — modern, 1986

Camp Loon Point, September 1986. The campers went home on Monday, the camp has been sold, and on Tuesday the bulldozers come for the boathouse. Eight counsellors stayed for one last night at the lake. Expect to play more than one character.

Fri · Afternoon · 5 players

The New Light

Cthulhu by Gaslight — 1895

The brightest house in three counties: two hundred and twelve electric lamps and not a candle on the premises. The engineer who installed it was found in the long gallery with no mark on him. The dark had to go somewhere.

Sat · Afternoon · 4 players

Paper Children

Blade Runner RPG

She sold the birth certificates of children who died in infancy to replicants who wanted to be somebody. Her killer will not be difficult. By this evening you will also have the list.

Sun · Morning · 5 players

The Favour

The Dee Sanction

Six months ago you left a witch finder at a crossroads with a list in his hand — three villages where the evidence didn’t add up and the verdict came anyway. Dee took the list. Now Garratt has called in the favour.

Sun · Afternoon · 4 players

The Perpetual Order

Cthulhu by Gaslight — 1895

A banker’s order has paid the rent on vault 114 every quarter since 1794. The wharf was built in 1829. Inside are a hundred and one identical crates, and every one of them is the same Friday in June.

Dead Air
A Call of Cthulhu trilogy · Thursday, Friday & Saturday evenings · 6 players

Under the Chancellery garden, below every level that appears on a captured plan, they built a room with walls the wrong shape. It was made to broadcast once, on the last night, to every loudspeaker still standing in Europe. They believed they were calling something. They were the call. Each part stands completely alone.

Thu · Evening · Part One

I. Ash & Concrete

Berlin, February 1946

Six weeks of frozen filing cabinets, then a requisition for forty tons of concrete delivered to a building that was already finished. There is a room under the bunker that is on nobody’s plan.

Fri · Evening · Part Two

II. The Quiet City

A closed city in the Urals, October 1962

On Monday the gates shut for an “administrative period”. On Tuesday a technician takes off his headphones and walks into the lake. On Friday, at six in the morning, someone is going to close a switch.

Sat · Evening · Part Three

III. Full Volume

Berlin, today

A lacquer disc sold at auction for four hundred euros to a producer who liked the label. He sampled fifteen seconds. You have almost certainly already heard it. At midnight it closes a festival, live, to two hundred thousand people.
