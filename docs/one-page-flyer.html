Wyrd Sisters Games Club presents

Contingency 2027

27–31 January 2027 · Searles Leisure Resort, Hunstanton · five days of gaming

Nine games, run by Tim Evans. Two a day — one afternoon, one evening. Four-hour slots, pre-generated characters, no system knowledge needed, safety tools at every table.

Wed · Afternoon · 6 players

Another Bloody Treasure Map

Vanity — a light house d6 game

You bought a treasure map off a man in a tavern. Yes. You know. So did four other groups this week, and by Tuesday there were three parties on the same road being extremely rude to each other. Here’s the thing, though: the map is real. The vault is where it says it is, there is a great deal of gold in it, and the old man who printed four hundred of them wasn’t running a swindle. He was recruiting.

Wed · Evening · 4 players

Afterimage

Blade Runner RPG

Los Angeles, 2037. A memory designer is dead in his studio and Rep-Detect has the case with a whisper attached: rogue replicant. The whisper comes from a corporation, and it is a lie of the useful kind. You have until dawn, the case you were handed is wrong in every particular, and nobody in this story gets to be right — you only get to decide who pays.

Thu · Afternoon · 5 players

Black Summer: Last Flight Out

The Walking Dead Universe RPG

New York, six weeks after the fall. The army’s last plane leaves an old Brooklyn airfield at dawn and boarding needs a stamped chit with your face on it. Between the five of you, you have three. The queue at the far end is longer than anyone wants to think about.

Fri · Afternoon · 5 players

The Favour

The Dee Sanction

Six months ago you left a witch finder at a crossroads with a list in his hand — three villages where the evidence didn’t add up and the verdict came anyway. Dee took the list. Now Garratt has called in the favour, and the village has its own view. A sequel that stands alone.

Sat · Afternoon · 4 players

The Curious Case of Mr Parker’s Diary

Vaesen — Nordic Horror

A parcel reaches the Society: the field diary of an English naturalist who drowned in a northern lake last October — posted three weeks after he died. The first sixty pages are ordinary. The last twenty are in the same hand and written from somewhere outside his own life. The final page is dated nine days from now, and it describes your arrival.

Sun · Afternoon · 4–6 players

Clean Ground

The Custodians — a house BRP game

In 1962 your department filed a spare valley in the northern march, in case a war made one necessary. It never cancelled the entry. Two weeks ago a contractor felled the tree the whole arrangement was pegged to, and there is a body in a sheep pen with boots resoled nine times by hand. Forty-one people have been walking north across that valley for sixty-four years. There is no north.

Dead Air
A Call of Cthulhu trilogy · Thursday, Friday & Saturday evenings · 6 players

Under the Chancellery garden, below every level that appears on a captured plan, they built a room. Its walls are the wrong shape. It was made to broadcast once, on the last night, to every loudspeaker still standing in Europe. They believed they were calling something. They were the call. Three eras, three investigations, an audience that keeps getting bigger — and each part stands completely alone.

Thu · Evening · Part One

I. Ash & Concrete

Berlin, February 1946

You catalogue German science before the other lot get to it, and it has been six weeks of frozen filing cabinets. Then a requisition turns up for forty tons of concrete, delivered to a building that was already finished. There is a room under the bunker that is on nobody’s plan.

Fri · Evening · Part Two

II. The Quiet City

A closed city in the Urals, October 1962

You have worked here eleven years and long ago stopped being curious about the tapes. On Monday the gates shut for an “administrative period”. On Tuesday a technician takes off his headphones and walks into the lake. On Friday, at six in the morning, someone is going to close a switch.

Sat · Evening · Part Three

III. Full Volume

Berlin, today

A lacquer disc came out of a box of Cold War acetates at auction and sold for four hundred euros to a producer who liked the label. He sampled fifteen seconds. You have almost certainly already heard it. At midnight it closes a festival, live, to two hundred thousand people.
