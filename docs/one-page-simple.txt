CONTINGENCY 2027
Wyrd Sisters Games Club - 27-31 January 2027 - Searles, Hunstanton

Twelve games run by Tim Evans, across nine systems. Four-hour slots, pregens
provided, no system knowledge needed, safety tools at every table. Booking on
Warhorn; full content notes on each game's listing.

THE PERPETUAL ORDER - Cthulhu by Gaslight (1895) - Wed morning - 4 players
A banker's order has paid the rent on vault 114 at a Wapping bonded wharf every
quarter since 1794. The wharf was built in 1829. Nobody has ever opened it, and
between the four of you the padlock takes nine seconds. Inside are a hundred and
one identical crates, and every one is the same Friday in June.

AFTERIMAGE - Blade Runner RPG - Wed afternoon - 4 players
Los Angeles, 2037. A memory designer is dead and Rep-Detect has the case with a
whisper attached: rogue replicant. The whisper comes from a corporation, and it
is a lie of the useful kind. Nobody here gets to be right. You only get to decide
who pays.

THE SIX O'CLOCK SITTING - Cthulhu by Gaslight (1894) - Wed evening - 4 players
You have done eleven of these, and Mrs Veale will not be difficult. She isn't. By
seven you have the cabinet, the thread and the whole shabby machine. What you do
not have is how she knew the contents of a letter that was never posted.

BLACK SUMMER: LAST FLIGHT OUT - Walking Dead Universe - Thu morning - 5 players
New York, six weeks after the fall. The army's last plane leaves a Brooklyn
airfield at dawn and boarding needs a stamped chit with your face on it. Between
the five of you, you have three. The queue at the far end is longer than anybody
wants to think about.

CONTINUOUS SERVICE - ALIEN RPG - Thu afternoon - 5 players
The ore hauler Keelson stopped answering eleven years ago. Your tug has the
salvage contract: board her, verify the manifest, rig the tow. The manifest
verifies. So does the payroll - forty-one crew, all clocked in, wages accruing
since 2175, every shift paid without anyone once asking why. Somebody aboard is
still filing the timesheets, and she has a reason.

THE LONDON PARTICULAR - Cthulhu by Gaslight (1896) - Thu evening - 5 players
Four people have died in four weeks, each on a night of thick fog, each found in
the open street with no wound on them. Nothing connects them until somebody
fetches the Meteorological Office's fog register, and then it connects them
perfectly: each died exactly where the fog lay thickest. Tonight's forecast is
the worst of the winter, and it will not lift before dawn.

VIVA VOCE - Call of Cthulhu, 1920s - Fri afternoon - 5 players
Miskatonic University, March 1927. A doctoral thesis of four hundred and six
pages on the drainage of the Miskatonic valley. Three examiners have read it: one
is in the Sanatorium and will not stop writing, one has gone deaf to a single
specific sound, and the third is perfectly well, which is the one that should
worry you. The viva is at three o'clock, and the committee must vote.

THE CURIOUS CASE OF MR PARKER'S DIARY - Vaesen - Fri evening - 4 players
The Mythic North, February 1901. A sawmill parish in northern Sweden, in the
fortnight of the year when the lake is thickest, and a diary whose last twenty
pages are in Mr Parker's hand. He has been dead a month.

PAPER CHILDREN - Blade Runner RPG - Sat afternoon - 4 players
A records clinician is dead on her own floor with fourteen years of immaculate
filing around her. She sold the birth certificates of children who died in
infancy to replicants who wanted to be somebody. Her killer will not be
difficult. By this evening you will also have the list.

CLOSING WEEKEND - Call of Cthulhu, modern rules, 1986 - Sat evening - 6 players
Camp Loon Point, Saturday 6 September 1986. The campers went home on Monday, the
camp has been sold to a developer, and on Tuesday the bulldozers come for the
boathouse. Eight counsellors stayed behind to close the cabins and have one last
night at the lake. This is a slasher and it is played like one: the phone line
turns out to be cut rather than down, the wagon will not start, and you should
expect to play more than one character.

THE FAVOUR - The Dee Sanction - Sun morning - 5 players
Six months ago you left a witch finder at a crossroads with a list in his hand:
three villages where the evidence did not add up and the verdict came anyway. Dee
took the list. Now Garratt has called in the favour, and what he wants is one name
cleared - a woman he hanged in 1581 on evidence he was paid to believe.

PROFESSIONAL COURTESY - Outgunned - Sun afternoon - 5 players
Atlantic City, September 1988. The Labor Day take from four casinos is in the
vault at Boardwalk Fidelity until Tuesday, and you have spent eleven weeks on
this: the guard rota, the alarm loop, a man inside, ninety seconds of thermal
lance. What you do not have is the vault, because another crew came through the
roof nine minutes ago and they look just as surprised as you do.
