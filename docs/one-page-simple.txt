CONTINGENCY 2027
Wyrd Sisters Games Club - 27-31 January 2027 - Searles, Hunstanton

Twelve games run by Tim Evans. Four-hour slots, pregens provided, no system
knowledge needed, safety tools at every table. Booking on Warhorn; full content
notes on each game's listing.

THE LAST FIX - Pulp Cthulhu - Wed morning - 5 players
July 1937, the central Pacific. You are the search party for Amelia Earhart. On
day nine you find the aircraft, intact, high up a strand, with the hatch open
from the inside. On day ten you find what it came down to look at, and why every
chart aboard has been wrong all along.

AFTERIMAGE - Blade Runner RPG - Wed afternoon - 4 players
Los Angeles, 2037. A memory designer is dead and Rep-Detect has the case with a
whisper attached: rogue replicant. The whisper comes from a corporation, and it
is a lie of the useful kind. Nobody here gets to be right. You only get to decide
who pays.

THE SIX O'CLOCK SITTING - Cthulhu by Gaslight (1894) - Wed evening - 4 players
You have done eleven of these, and Mrs Veale will not be difficult. She isn't. By
seven you have the cabinet, the thread and the whole shabby machine. What you do
not have is how she knew the contents of a letter that was never posted.

BLACK SUMMER: LAST FLIGHT OUT - Walking Dead Universe - Thu morning - 5 players
New York, six weeks after the fall. The army's last plane leaves a Brooklyn
airfield at dawn and boarding needs a stamped chit with your face on it. Between
the five of you, you have three. The queue at the far end is longer than anybody
wants to think about.

THE SKY RIDE - Pulp Cthulhu - Thu afternoon - 5 players
Chicago, November 1933. The World's Fair has nine days left and a hundred
thousand people a day coming through the gates, and eleven of them have gone
missing since July. Two turned up in sealed places. The Fair is not closing, and
on the thirteenth every crate in it goes by rail to nineteen cities.

THE NEW LIGHT - Cthulhu by Gaslight (1895) - Fri afternoon - 5 players
The brightest house in three counties: two hundred and twelve electric lamps and
not a candle on the premises. The engineer who installed it was found in the long
gallery with no mark on him. The dark had to go somewhere.

PAPER CHILDREN - Blade Runner RPG - Sat afternoon - 4 players
A records clinician is dead on her own floor with fourteen years of immaculate
filing around her. She sold the birth certificates of children who died in
infancy to replicants who wanted to be somebody. Her killer will not be
difficult. By this evening you will also have the list.

THE FAVOUR - The Dee Sanction - Sun morning - 5 players
Six months ago you left a witch finder at a crossroads with a list in his hand:
three villages where the evidence did not add up and the verdict came anyway. Dee
took the list. Now Garratt has called in the favour, and what he wants is one name
cleared - a woman he hanged in 1581 on evidence he was paid to believe.

THE PERPETUAL ORDER - Cthulhu by Gaslight (1895) - Sun afternoon - 4 players
A banker's order has paid the rent on vault 114 at a Wapping bonded wharf every
quarter since 1794. The wharf was built in 1829. Nobody has ever opened it, and
between the four of you the padlock takes nine seconds. Inside are a hundred and
one identical crates, and every one is the same Friday in June.

DEAD AIR - a Call of Cthulhu trilogy - Thu, Fri and Sat evenings - 6 players
Under the Chancellery garden, below every level that appears on a captured plan,
they built a room with walls the wrong shape. It was made to broadcast once, on
the last night, to every loudspeaker still standing in Europe. They believed they
were calling something. They were the call. Each part stands completely alone.

I. ASH & CONCRETE - Berlin, February 1946 - Thu evening
Six weeks of frozen filing cabinets, and then a requisition for forty tons of
concrete delivered to a building that was already finished. There is a room under
the bunker that is on nobody's plan, it is under two metres of water, and you are
not the only people who want it.

II. THE QUIET CITY - a closed city in the Urals, October 1962 - Fri evening
You have worked here eleven years and long ago stopped being curious about the
tapes. On Monday the gates shut for an administrative period. On Tuesday a
technician takes off his headphones and walks into the lake. On Friday, at six in
the morning, someone is going to close a switch.

III. FULL VOLUME - Berlin, today - Sat evening
A lacquer disc came out of a box of Cold War acetates at auction and sold for
four hundred euros to a producer who liked the label. He sampled fifteen seconds.
You have almost certainly already heard it. At midnight it closes a festival,
live and uncut, to two hundred thousand people.
