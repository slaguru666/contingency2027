**CONTINGENCY 2027** · Wyrd Sisters Games Club
27–31 January 2027 · Searles Leisure Resort, Hunstanton · five days of gaming

**Eight games, run by Tim Evans.** Two a day — one afternoon, one evening.
Four-hour slots, pre-generated characters, no system knowledge needed, safety
tools at every table.

---

**BLACK SUMMER: LAST FLIGHT OUT** — The Walking Dead Universe RPG
*Wed afternoon · 5 players*
New York, six weeks after the fall. The army's last plane leaves an old Brooklyn
airfield at dawn and boarding needs a stamped chit with your face on it. Between
the five of you, you have three. The queue at the far end is longer than anyone
wants to think about.

**AFTERIMAGE** — Blade Runner RPG
*Wed evening · 4 players*
Los Angeles, 2037. A memory designer is dead in his studio and Rep-Detect has the
case with a whisper attached: *rogue replicant*. The whisper comes from a
corporation, and it is a lie of the useful kind. Nobody here gets to be right —
you only decide who pays.

**THE FAVOUR** — The Dee Sanction
*Thu evening · 5 players*
Six months ago you left a witch finder at a crossroads with a list in his hand —
three villages where the evidence didn't add up and the verdict came anyway. Dee
took the list. Now Garratt has called in the favour, and the village has its own
view. A sequel that stands alone.

**ANOTHER BLOODY TREASURE MAP** — Vanity, a light house d6 game
*Fri evening · 5 players*
You bought a treasure map off a man in a tavern. Yes. You know. So did four other
groups this week, and by Tuesday there were three parties on the same road being
extremely rude to each other. Here's the thing, though: the map is real. The
vault is where it says it is, there is a great deal of gold in it, and the old
man who printed four hundred of them wasn't running a swindle. He was recruiting.

**THE CURIOUS CASE OF MR PARKER'S DIARY** — Vaesen, Nordic Horror
*Sat evening · 4 players*
A parcel reaches the Society: the field diary of an English naturalist who
drowned in a northern lake last October — posted three weeks after he died. The
first sixty pages are ordinary. The last twenty are in the same hand, plainly and
unmistakably his, and they are written from somewhere outside his own life. The
final page is dated nine days from now, and it describes your arrival.

---

## DEAD AIR — a Call of Cthulhu trilogy
*Thursday, Friday & Saturday afternoons · 5 players*

One pattern, pressed onto lacquer in the last winter of the war. Every government
that found it was certain it was a weapon. **It was an advertisement.** Three
eras, three investigations, and an audience that keeps getting bigger. **Each
part stands completely alone** — take one, take two, or take all three.

**I. ASH & AMBER** — Berlin, February 1946 *(Thu afternoon)*
You catalogue German science before the other lot get to it, and it has been six
weeks of frozen filing cabinets. Then an address turns out to be a broadcasting
annexe that officially never existed, with a sub-basement somebody deliberately
flooded. Two armies are digging for the same room.

**II. THE QUIET CITY** — a closed city in the Urals, October 1962 *(Fri afternoon)*
You have worked here eleven years and long ago stopped being curious about the
tapes. On Monday the gates shut for an "administrative period". On Tuesday a
technician takes off his headphones and walks into the lake. On Friday, at six in
the morning, someone is going to close a switch.

**III. FULL VOLUME** — Berlin, today *(Sat afternoon)*
A lacquer disc came out of a box of Cold War acetates at auction and sold for
four hundred euros to a producer who liked the label. He sampled fifteen seconds.
You have almost certainly already heard it. At midnight it closes a festival,
uncut, live, to two hundred thousand people.
