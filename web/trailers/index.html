Wyrd Sisters Games Club presents

Contingency 2027

27–31 January 2027 · Searles Leisure Resort, Hunstanton · five days of gaming

Twelve games across nine systems, run by Tim Evans. Four-hour slots, pre-generated characters, no system knowledge needed, safety tools at every table. Thirty seconds each — none of them give the game away.

Wednesday 27 January

The Perpetual Order

Cthulhu by Gaslight — 1895

Morning · 4-6 players

A banker's order has paid the rent on vault 114 at a Wapping bonded wharf every quarter since 1794. The wharf was built in 1829. The order has never missed a payment, nobody at the bank can say who signs it, and nobody has ever opened the door. Tonight the rent falls due.

Also as 9:16 · 1:1 GARROW'S BONDED WHARF, WAPPING · 1895

Afterimage

Blade Runner RPG

Afternoon · 4 players

Los Angeles, 2037. A memory designer is dead and Rep-Detect has the case with a whisper attached: rogue replicant. Somebody wanted you to hear that word before you ever saw the room. Nobody here gets to be right. You only get to decide who pays.

Also as 9:16 · 1:1 LOS ANGELES · 2037

The Six O'Clock Sitting

Cthulhu by Gaslight — 1894

Evening · 4-6 players

You have done eleven of these, and Mrs Veale will not be difficult. She isn't. By seven you have the cabinet, the thread and the whole shabby machine. What you do not have is how she knew the contents of a letter that was never posted.

Also as 9:16 · 1:1 PIMLICO, LONDON · NOVEMBER 1894

Thursday 28 January

Black Summer

The Walking Dead Universe — Last Flight Out

Morning · 4-6 players

New York, six weeks after the fall. The army's last plane leaves a Brooklyn airfield at dawn and boarding needs a stamped chit with your face on it. There are three chits, and there are more of you than that. The queue at the far end is longer than anybody wants to think about.

Also as 9:16 · 1:1 NEW YORK · SIX WEEKS AFTER THE FALL

Continuous Service

ALIEN RPG — Cinematic play

Afternoon · 4-6 players

The ore hauler Keelson stopped answering eleven years ago. Your tug has the salvage contract: board her, verify the manifest, rig the tow. The manifest verifies. So does the payroll - forty-one crew, all clocked in, wages accruing since 2175, every shift paid without anyone once asking why. Somebody aboard is still filing the timesheets.

Also as 9:16 · 1:1 THE ORE HAULER KEELSON · 2186

The London Particular

Cthulhu by Gaslight — 1896

Evening · 4-6 players

Four people have died in four weeks, each on a night of thick fog, each found in the open street with no wound on them. Nothing connects them - not trade, not street, not parish, not kin - and the only office in London that keeps a record of where the fog lay is the Meteorological Office. Tonight's forecast is the worst of the winter, and it will not lift before dawn.

Also as 9:16 · 1:1 LONDON · DECEMBER 1896

Friday 29 January

Viva Voce

Call of Cthulhu 7e — 1920s

Afternoon · 4-6 players

Miskatonic University, March 1927. A doctoral thesis of four hundred and six pages on the drainage of the Miskatonic valley. Three examiners have read it: one is in the Sanatorium and will not stop writing, one has gone deaf to a single specific sound, and the third is perfectly well, which is the one that should worry you. The viva is at three o'clock, and the committee must vote.

Also as 9:16 · 1:1 MISKATONIC UNIVERSITY, ARKHAM · MARCH 1927

Mr Parker's Diary

Vaesen — Nordic Horror

Evening · 4-6 players

The Mythic North, February 1901. A sawmill parish in northern Sweden, in the fortnight of the year when the lake is thickest, and a diary whose last twenty pages are in Mr Parker's hand. He has been dead a month.

Also as 9:16 · 1:1 HÄLLVATTNET, NORTHERN SWEDEN · FEBRUARY 1901

Saturday 30 January

Paper Children

Blade Runner RPG

Afternoon · 4 players

A records clinician is dead on her own floor with fourteen years of immaculate filing around her. Her killer will not be difficult, and you will have a name by evening. It is what she filed that you will not be able to give back.

Also as 9:16 · 1:1 LOS ANGELES · 2037

Closing Weekend

Call of Cthulhu — modern rules, 1986

Evening · 4-6 players

Camp Loon Point, Saturday 6 September 1986. The campers went home on Monday, the camp has been sold to a developer, and on Tuesday the bulldozers come for the boathouse. The counsellors stayed behind to close the cabins and have one last night at the lake. This is a slasher and it is played like one: the phone line turns out to be cut rather than down, the wagon will not start, and you should expect to play more than one character.

Also as 9:16 · 1:1 CAMP LOON POINT · SEPTEMBER 1986

Sunday 31 January

The Favour

The Dee Sanction

Morning · 4-6 players

Six months ago you left a witch finder at a crossroads with a list in his hand: three villages where the evidence did not add up and the verdict came anyway. Dee took the list. Now Garratt has called in the favour, and what he wants is one name cleared - a woman he hanged in 1581 on evidence he was paid to believe.

Also as 9:16 · 1:1 SKELTHWAITE, WEST RIDING · APRIL 1587

Provenance

Outgunned

Afternoon · 4-6 players

Aragon, Spain, October 1938. The Ebro front is collapsing, and a German survey column with Condor Legion papers has spent nine days in a dry riverbed sixty kilometres behind the line. They are not digging anything up. They have brought something with them - a twelfth-century reliquary, bought at auction in Vienna in 1932, with an export licence, a bill of sale, and a provenance file proving it was looted from this valley by Napoleon's quartermasters in 1811. They are putting it back, and they are entirely within their rights.

Also as 9:16 · 1:1 BARRANCO DE SANT GRAU, ARAGÓN · OCTOBER 1938
